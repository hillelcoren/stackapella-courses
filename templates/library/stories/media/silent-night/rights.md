# Silent Night: image rights evidence

Checked 2026-11-12. Originals in raw/; finals made with sips only (BMP round trip strips metadata).

## 1.jpg: Oberndorf in the 1899 flood

- Item: "Diapositiv: Ortsansicht, Hochwasser 1899, Oberndorf bei Salzburg, Land Salzburg", glass lantern slide,
  black and white, 8.5 x 8.5 cm. Inventory number dia/1838. Inscription on the slide: "1838. // 806 // MUSEUM FÜR
  VOLKSKUNDE // Oberndorf bei Salzburg, Kirche, Ansicht bei Hochwasser (1899)"; verso "C I e 9". Former owner:
  Verein Deutsche Heimat; acquired as a gift.
- Holder: Volkskundemuseum Wien. Item page: https://sammlung.volkskundemuseum.at/id/332540
  IIIF manifest: https://sammlung.volkskundemuseum.at/i3f/v20/332540/manifest
  Downloaded file: https://sammlung.volkskundemuseum.at/i3f/v20/336522/full/full/0/default.jpg (4378 x 4321),
  saved as raw/vkm-dia1838.jpg; the final crops away the slide's black frame and paper label.
- Rights statement, exact words on the item page, under each image: "Volkskundemuseum Wien CC PDM 1.0", linking to
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/
- Kulturpool (Austria's national aggregator) record ac3b8020-5a95-4e1d-8b77-001c967e5dcb:
  "edmRights": "http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/", "edmRightsName": "Public Domain",
  "edmRightsReusePolicy": "OPEN". https://api.kulturpool.at/search/?q=Oberndorf%20bei%20Salzburg%20Diapositiv
- Museum metadata: creator "unbekannt" (unknown); "Entstehung: Wien (Aufnahmeort); Oberndorf bei Salzburg
  (Aufnahmeort Vorlage); 1899 (Aufnahmedatum)". That is, the original photograph was taken at Oberndorf in 1899 and
  the slide was made from it in Vienna (Kulturpool dates its sister slide dia/1839 "vor 1937").
- Public domain by age:
  - A photograph of 1899 by an unknown photographer.
  - Austria (country of origin) and the EU: an anonymous work's term is 70 years from when it was made available, or
    from creation if unpublished, so it ended by 1969 at the latest. A simple photograph (Lichtbild, UrhG § 74) has
    50 years. The slide itself, a photographic copy made before 1937, would also be long out of any Lichtbild term.
  - US: if published before 1931 it is PD; if unpublished, an anonymous work's term is 120 years from creation, which
    ended at the close of 2019.
  - Israel: anonymous works 70 years from publication or creation, and photographs made under the old law 50 years
    from the negative; both long expired.
- What it shows: houses deep in floodwater, the Salzach, a domed building on the hill at the top left and a white
  church with a tall tower in the distance. No people visible; no drawn figures; no logos or watermarks.
- Caption facts: Silent Night Society, Oberndorf page, https://www.stillenacht.at/en/sites/oberndorf : "Fires and
  floods (the bridge to Laufen was torn away in 1899)" were reasons for moving the town up the Salzach from 1901;
  "The church of St. Nikola in the old part of the village was demolished"; "In 1937 the Silent Night Chapel was
  built in its place." The caption does not claim that the church in the photo is St Nicholas. The slide's label
  says only "Kirche" (church), and the domed hilltop building may be another church.

## 2.jpg: The Sunday School Service and Tune Book (1863), p. 34, No. 15

- Item: The Sunday school service and tune book, compiled by John C. Hollister (New York: Mason Brothers [etc.],
  1863). Silent Night is No. 15, page 34, headed "Silent Night! Holy Night!" and marked "From the Third
  (unpublished) Part of 'Hymns and Music for the Young.' By permission of the Author." The page is in 6/8, four
  parts on two staves, with three verses.
- Holder of the copy: Princeton Theological Seminary Library (IA metadata "contributor"); scanned by the Internet
  Archive in 2011 (sponsor Calvin College). Item: https://archive.org/details/sundayschoolserv00holl ; page:
  https://archive.org/details/sundayschoolserv00holl/page/n35/mode/1up ; file
  https://archive.org/download/sundayschoolserv00holl/page/n35.jpg (2077 x 1793), saved as
  raw/ia-sundayschool-1863-p34.jpg. OCLC 1041776022. The final is the whole page, resized.
- Rights: the IA record states no licence, so this rests on PD by age, with the reasoning below:
  - Published in New York in 1863. The book's own notice reads "Entered according to Act of Congress, A. D. 1863",
    and its preface is dated September 1863.
  - The people involved all died long ago: John C. Hollister (compiler) 1818 to 1903, per Princeton Theological
    Seminary's catalogue as given in search results. John Freeman Young (English words) died in 1885. Franz Xaver
    Gruber (tune) died in 1863.
  - US: published before 1931, so it is PD.
  - EU and Israel: life plus 70; every author died in 1903 or before, so it is PD.
  - A faithful scan of a flat printed page adds no new copyright: in the US under Bridgeman v. Corel, and in the EU
    under Art. 14 of the 2019 DSM Directive and for lack of originality.
- What it shows: printed music and words only. No figures or ornaments with people, and no logos or watermarks.
- Caption facts: Hymnary.org, text page Notes: "John F. Young, first published in John C. Hollister's Sunday School
  Service and Tune Book (1863)." https://hymnary.org/text/silent_night_holy_night_all_is_calm_all . The story
  already cites this as [^hymnary-text]. The three verses on the page match Young's 1859 text as quoted in the story.

## Rejected

- Joseph Mohr's autograph, about 1820 to 1825 (Salzburg Museum, BIB HS 1814-97 per Hymnology Archive): the
  manuscript is PD, but no scan has a clear free statement.
  - Commons File:Autograph_Mohr_Stille_Nacht.jpg comes from a parish website (basilika-mariapfarr.at), not from the
    holder.
  - File:Autograph_Mohr_Stille_Nacht.png came via stillenacht.at.
  - The Salzburg Museum's "Photograph orders | Rights of use" page (https://www.salzburgmuseum.at/en/photograph-orders/)
    says "The rights of use of the available images remain in every case with Salzburg Museum" and grants them
    "only for the agreed purpose".
  - The museum's teaching PDF carries "© Salzburg Museum".
  - Art Resource sells a photo of a Silent Night manuscript from the Museum Carolino Augusteum, by Erich Lessing.
  - Austria protects even plain photographs (Lichtbilder) for 50 years, and the EU's Art. 14 exemption covers
    visual art, which a manuscript arguably is not. Not clearly OK, so dropped.
- Gruber's autographs: dropped.
  - Commons File:Autograph_Gruber_VII_Stille_Nacht.jpg is a halftone scan taken via meinbezirk.at and the Stille
    Nacht Gesellschaft. It has a Salzburg museum stamp, so it falls under the same terms as above.
  - File:Stille_nacht.jpg (about 1860) is a 2005 user photo with "Unknown source" and an unnamed holder.
- "Vier ächte Tyroler Lieder" (Friese, Dresden, about 1833):
  - Commons File:Tyroler_Lieder.jpg (600 x 499, via musikland-tirol.at) is the title page. It shows a drawn, coloured
    group of the four Strasser singers, so it breaks the no-drawn-people rule; its source is also unclear and it is
    low resolution.
  - The copies at the Morgan Library and the Deutscher Alpenverein library (55 C 1801) have no free licence statement
    online.
- Young, Carols for Christmas Tide (1859): the only scan found is on hymnsandcarolsofchristmas.com, of a copy stamped
  "Harris Collection", with no holder's licence. No IA, HathiTrust or Brown Digital Repository copy was found.
- Silent Night Chapel (a building): every Commons photo is CC BY-SA, or CC BY 2.5, 3.0 or 3.0 de. None is CC0,
  PD or CC BY 4.0.
- Library of Congress (loc.gov) pages could not be reached from here (Cloudflare challenge), so no LoC photo of
  Oberndorf or Arnsdorf was checked.
- Volkskundemuseum dia/1839 (Oberndorf with Laufen, before 1937) and dia/1840 (a second 1899 flood view) are both
  PDM and usable. They were left out because 1839's main church is Laufen's and 1840 nearly repeats 1838.
