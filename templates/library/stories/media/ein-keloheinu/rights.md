# Ein Keloheinu: image rights evidence

Checked 2026-11-13. Originals in raw/; finals made with sips only (BMP round trip strips metadata; crops with
sips -c and --cropOffset on the BMP, then -Z 1600 to JPEG at quality 82). Every original and every final crop was
opened and looked at. Check crops and bands are in check/.

Jewish-page rule (CHECK-NOTES-batch8): no full divine name in any final image, in Hebrew or in transliteration. The
hymn's first line of every verse holds the name (En kelohenu, mi chelohenu, node lelohenu, baruch elohenu, ata hu
elohenu), and both music sources print it in the heading and in the words. Each music crop below keeps only lines
with malkenu and moshienu; neither keeps Adonenu either (not a divine name, but left out to be safe). Exact lines
kept are listed per image.

  This leaf carries no Google mark and no library stamp.
- Public domain by age: published in the US in 1897, PD there. The tune is Freudenthal's (died 1874); the harmony is
  the hymnal committee's, published without a name 129 years ago (EU and Israel: an anonymous work's 70 years from
