## Rejected or not used

- A portrait of Newton: every likeness is a painting or engraving (no photographs; he died in 1807). Not allowed.
- Columbian Harmony (Cincinnati, 1829), the tune's earliest known printing: no free scan of the page was found on
  IA; not used.
- Newton's sermon notes (Lambeth Palace Library MS 2940): images shown only by Museum of the Bible, with no free
  licence. Not used.
- Excell's Make His Praise Glorious (1900) and Coronation Hymns (1910): probably usable PD pages (not checked in
  detail), but a fifth printed page would crowd the set; left out.
- Commons audio "File:Amazing grace 1922.ogg": its own page gives an old lcweb2 address as source and {{PD-US}} only; the
  recording's holder page at the Library of Congress is linked instead (links.md).
