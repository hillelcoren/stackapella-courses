  - Licence section, exact wikitext: "{{PD-art|PD-old-100-1923}}". Artist field "B. May [?]" (B. May was the Havana
    publisher of the album, not a named artist).
- Public domain by age:
  - Printed 1859 (some catalogues date the album a few years earlier; every date is in the 1850s). No draughtsman or
    lithographer is named on the plate.
  - US: published before 1931, so PD.
  - EU and Israel: life plus 70. Anyone who drew or printed it as an adult in the 1850s died long before 1956, so the

    Houghton claims none.
- Looked at in full and in zoomed crops (work/hz1.jpg, work/hz2.jpg): ship, sea, lighthouse, fortress, bastion with a
  sentry box, flags. No human figures anywhere, on deck or on the walls. No logos or watermarks.
- Words on the image: "ISLA DE CUBA" (printed "JSLA"), "MORRO Y ENTRADA DEL PUERTO DE LA HABANA", a plate number "1".
- Not a photograph, so the one-photo rule does not apply. Not used on any other song page (grep of state/media for
  43-1484, Morro, Havana, Habana, Cuba found nothing).
- Caption facts: "Cuando salí de la Habana" is the first line (story, [^century1906]); the album is "published in
  Berlin" (Houghton); "in the 1850s" hedges the album's exact year. The caption does not tie it to the song's own date.

## 2.jpg: Le Ménestrel, 25 September 1864, p. 344, top of the page

- Item: *Le Ménestrel*, 31st year (Paris: Heugel), p. 344, "Primes 1864-1865 du Ménestrel", "Fleurs d'Espagne",
  25 songs "del maestro Yradier", No. 14 "La Paloma (Colombe)", "Paroles de MM. Paul Bernard & Tagliafico". The
  story's [^menestrel1864] (25 September 1864, leaf 352).
- Holder: Boston Public Library copy (IA metadata: contributor "Boston Public Library", source "bplmusic", sponsor
  University of Ottawa). Not a Google scan; no watermark. IA record gives no licence field; status rests on age.
- File: https://archive.org/download/lemnestrel31pari/page/n351.jpg (2373 x 3517; leaf 352 counted from 1), saved as
  raw/menestrel_n351.jpg. The final is the top 1048 rows (page number 344 to the end of the Fleurs d'Espagne list),
  cropped before "Ou deux volumes des chansons de Gustave Nadaud"; work/men_crop.jpg.
- Public domain by age: printed Paris 1864. Makers named in the crop: J. L. Heugel, publisher and director, died 1883;
  Paul Bernard died 1879; D. Tagliafico died 1900 (IA name record "1821-1900"); Iradier died 1865. Unsigned
  advertisement text. US: before 1931. EU and Israel: life plus 70 ended by 1971 at the latest. Faithful scan.
- Every word in the crop read: the journal's masthead lines and subscription notice, "Les primes gratuites", and the
  25 song titles (Ay Chiquita, La Calesera, El Arreglito, ... La Gitana mexicaine, ... Morena (los Caracoles),
  Le Regard de ma blonde, Fête des Toreros, La Robe Azur). Nothing demeaning. A small inkblot left of "grands
  compositeurs" is in the print. The rest of the page (Duprez, Minkous, Gounod's 20 mélodies, Diémer) is cut away.
- No figures, ornaments are type only ("CHANT", "YRADIER" in decorated letters).

## Rejected

- Century Music 1906 edition, p. 2 (the arrangement's own source, BYU, archive.org lapalomadovecanc00yrad): carries a
  previous owner's pencilled English gloss by an unknown hand and of unknown date; its status is not clearly settled,
  and the page duplicates 3.jpg. Its cover is a "Vocal Blossoms" series list, not this song. Not used; the whole item
  is the "original" link instead.
- Boletín bibliográfico español, 1861, p. 122, entry 917 (archive.org boletnbibliogrf00hidagoog, and the Harvard copy
  boletnbibliogrf00piergoog): both are Google scans whose usage page asks "that you use these files for personal,
  non-commercial purposes" and not to remove the watermark; neither IA record carries a PD Mark. Dropped under the
  "personal/research use" rule (as BSB's Google scan was for another song). It stays a Sources link.
- El Arreglito, Paris cover (McGill F944): a drawn portrait of a singer fills the cover, and it is another song. Dropped.
- Library of Congress photographs: "Harbor entrance, Havana ... Morro Castle in foreground" (LCCN 91783889, about
  1890; 3c04108u.tif looked at in zoomed crops) shows small standing figures on the rocks and near the shore; "Harbor
  and Muelle Lus" (LCCN 93514455, 1904) shows people and carts on the quay; "Morro Castle ... ship on right"
  (LCCN 89707770) is a drawing with people on the Malecón. All dropped (no people in photos).
- NYPL "Harbor Entrance, Havana, Cuba" (Detroit Publishing postcard 5892, 1900): its rights statement is US-only
  ("did not make a determination as to its copyright status under the copyright laws of other countries"). Dropped
  in favour of the 1850s print.
- Carol M. Highsmith's Havana photographs (LoC, 2010): modern, not historical. Not used.
