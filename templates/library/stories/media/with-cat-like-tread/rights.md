# With Cat-like Tread: image rights evidence

Checked 2026-11-13. Originals in raw/ (Internet Archive page images, plain curl, one at a time); item metadata saved
in pages/*-metadata.json. Finals made with sips only (BMP round trip strips metadata; the one crop with sips -c and
--cropOffset on the BMP, then -Z 1600 to JPEG at quality 82). Every final was opened and looked at, and every printed
word on it read.

All four images are scans of flat printed pages, public domain by age. A faithful scan of a flat page adds no new
copyright: in the US under Bridgeman v. Corel, in the EU under Art. 14 of the 2019 DSM Directive and for lack of
originality; Israel likewise requires originality.

Makers and terms: W. S. Gilbert died 29 May 1911 (LoC NAF n80045872; London Remembers), Arthur Sullivan died 1900
(LoC NAF n80019543). Life plus 70 (EU, Israel) ended 1981 and 1970; life plus 100 (Mexico) ended 2011 and 2000. US:
all published before 1931. No arranger, editor or other contributor is named on any page used or on the title pages;
unsigned publisher's matter (stage directions as printed, page heads, plate numbers) published by 1925 is out of term
everywhere, Mexico's 100 years from publication for anonymous works included.

## 3.jpg: Stoddart vocal score (1880), Act II, No. 12, p. 121

- Same item as 1.jpg, image n122. Story: [^stoddart-score], pp. 121 to 124, images n122 to n125.
- Every word read: the stage direction across the top (as in 2.jpg, ending "The King is laden with burglarious tools
  and pistols, etc. etc."), "No 12  Solo, Samuel, & Chorus of Pirates." (printed with a dash after "Solo"), "Chorus
  of Pirates", "Allegro marziale.", "Piano.", the sung words "With cat-like tread upon our prey we steal; In silence
  dread our cautious way we feel! No sound at all, we never speak a word; A fly's foot-fall would be distinctly
  heard!", "Chorus of Police. Ta-ran-ta-ra, ta-ran-ta-ra!", "So stealthily the Pirate creeps, While all the household
  soundly sleeps.", the signature mark "16" and page number "121". Nothing that mocks anyone; no figures. Full page.

## 4.jpg: Chappell vocal score (London, catalogued [1920?]), p. 152

- Item: https://archive.org/details/vocalscoreofpira00sull , image n159. Record: title "Vocal score of The pirates
  of Penzance, or, The slave of duty", publisher "London : Chappell", date "1920", contributor University of Toronto,
  Faculty of Music Library, call number "M1503 .S949 P5 1920" (no copyright-status field). The title page (image n4,
  not used) cites "the Copyright Act, 1911", so this printing is 1911 or later; catalogued 1920. US: published
  before 1931. Story: [^chappell-score], the score our arrangement is read from.
- Every word read: "152", the pirates' "With a little burglaree! With cat-like tread Upon our prey we steal; In
  silence dread Our cautious way we feel.", the police's "ra. Ta-ran-ta-ra, ra, ra, ta-ran-ta-ra ... ra, ra, ra!",
  rehearsal letter "E", dynamics, plate number "23731". A reader's long pencil stroke crosses the last system (left
  in, described in the alt text). Nothing that mocks anyone; no figures. Full page.
- The title page itself is not used: it carries a performing-rights notice naming Rupert D'Oyly Carte (died 1948),
  and is not needed.

## Rejected

- Hail! Hail! The Gang's All Here (Leo Feist, 1917), cover and music pages (University of Arizona): words by "D. A.
  Esrom", a pen name of Theodore and Theodora Morse; Theodora Morse died 1953, so under life plus 100 (Mexico) the
  words are protected until 2053. Dropped (not looked at further).
- Gilbert's Bab drawings, posters, playbills and production photographs: all show people.
- Fifth Avenue Theatre, New York (Commons): "Fifthavetheat.JPG" is an engraving from a clipping; "Interior view of
  Augustin Daly's Fifth Avenue Theatre" (NYPL) is a print; "Fifthavenuetheat1899.jpg" shows the theatre rebuilt
  after the 1891 fire, not the 1879 house. None used.
- Opera Comique and the Royal Bijou Theatre, Paignton: no free photograph found (Commons categories empty; Geograph
  photos of Paignton are CC BY-SA). LoC and NYPL searches were blocked by bot checks.
- A photograph of a dark lantern or a "life preserver" club (Samuel's tools): I found no licence statement for the image of
  the Horniman object 99.35 the story cites; Met and Rijksmuseum searches found only ordinary lanterns, not ones
  recorded as dark lanterns. Not used rather than guessed.
- Hitchcock libretto cast page (image n7, "as first produced at the Fifth Avenue Theatre"): not needed; it is a list
  of performers' names.
