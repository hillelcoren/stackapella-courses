## 2.jpg: Sharp 1825, p. 113 (the songs' heading and Song II)

- Item: Thomas Sharp, A Dissertation on the Pageants or Dramatic Mysteries Anciently Performed at Coventry
  (Coventry: Merridew and Son, 1825). Internet Archive item adissertationonthepageants ("Book digitized by Google and
  uploaded by ARLIMA"), scan image n142 = printed p. 113 (the page carries no number; p. 114 follows on n143).
  https://archive.org/details/adissertationonthepageants/page/n142/mode/2up
  Downloaded: https://archive.org/download/adissertationonthepageants/page/n142.jpg (6267 x 8414), saved as
  raw/sharp-n142.jpg. The final is cropped to the printed text (902 x 1600); the "Digitized by Google" mark at the
  page foot is cropped away.
- Rights statement: IA metadata licenseurl "https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/"
  (https://archive.org/metadata/adissertationonthepageants/metadata).
- Public domain by age: printed 1825; Thomas Sharp died 1841 (DNB, cited in the story). US: published before 1931.
  UK/EU and Israel (life plus 70): expired 1911. The words of the songs are from a leaf dated 1591. A flat scan adds no
  new right.
- Every word on the final: "Theise Songes belonge to THE TAYLORS & SHEAREMENS PAGANT. THE FIRST AND THE LASTE THE
  SHEPHEARDS SINGE AND THE SECOND OR MIDDLEMOST THE WOMEN SINGE." / "Thomas Mawdycke" / "die decimo tertio Maij anno
  dni millesimo quingentesimo nonagesimo primo. Praetor fuit civitatis Coventriae D. Mathaeus Richardson tunc Consules
  Johannes Whitehead & Thomas Grauener." (black-letter, read from the scan) / "SONG I." As I out rode this enderes
  night / Of thre ioli sheppardes I saw a sight / And all a bowte there fold a star shone bright / They sange terli
  terlow / So mereli the sheppards ther pipes can blow / "SONG II." the refrain and verse 1 as the story prints them.
  Nothing anti-Jewish or demeaning; the play's dialogue (pp. 83 to 112) is not on this page.

## Considered and rejected

- Commons "Mitchell ViewOfCoventry HAGAM.jpg" (Herbert Art Gallery, hand-coloured etching "before 1800", no figures):
  free by age, but only 1000 px, its date is given only as "before 1800", and the Herbert's own record could not be
  found online (coventrycollections.org shows only its homepage). Hollar's plan is earlier and better documented.
  Kept in raw/ (commons-mitchell-view.jpg) in case the lead wants a fourth image.
- John Speed's 1610 Coventry plan (Cambridge University Library, via Commons): CUDL's download licence is CC BY-NC.
- Bradford's 1748/49 map of Coventry (Herbert, Commons): photo licensed CC BY-SA 3.0.
- Commons "Butchers Row in Coventry, 1890-1920.jpg": source is a blog; holder and photographer unknown.
- Commons "Birmingham Central Library fire jan1879.jpg": 480 px, tagged PD in the US only, source a dead council link.
- British Library Flickr scan of Poole's "Ancient Map of Coventry" (1870, PDM): a redrawing of the same plan; the
  Hollar original is preferred.
- Woodhouse, The Churches of Coventry (1909): photographs by Wilfred Sims, death year unknown, so not cleared in the EU.
- Sharp plate 117 ("Song II. continued", bass part): fine, but a second music plate adds little.
- Nothing from the pageant's dialogue pages, and no picture of the massacre or of any figure, was considered.
