# Row, Row, Row Your Boat: rights evidence

Checked 2026-11-13. Images 1, 2 and 4 are faithful scans of flat printed pages; image 3 is a photograph of buildings
with the Library of Congress's own rights statement. Every printed word on each crop was read (alts quote them in

## 1.jpg: The Teachers' Institute Glee Book (Lancaster, Pa., 1874), p. 39, "Row, Row. Round."
- Item: https://archive.org/details/teachersinstitut00hall/page/n40 (the story's earliestPrinting, [^hall1874]).
- IA metadata (https://archive.org/metadata/teachersinstitut00hall, saved in notes/): creator "Hall, W. B" and "Lyte,
  E. Oram (Eliphalet Oram), 1842-1913, author"; date 1874; publisher "Lancaster, PA : Hall & Lyte"; contributor "Music
  Library, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill"; no rights or licenseurl field.
- Makers and death years: the round on the crop is unsigned; its tune is credited to E. O. Lyte from 1881 and signed by
  him in 1893 (a listed maker, died 1913, LC authority no2018014532); its words are traditional (first two lines in
  print 1852). Co-preparer W. B. Hall: death year not found (searched); his name is not on the crop and no text or
  music on the crop is credited to him. Anonymous reading: published 1874, so 100 years from publication (Mexico) ended
  1974 and 70 years (EU, Israel) ended 1944.
- PD: published 1874. US: before 1931. EU, Israel (life+70) and Mexico (life+100): Lyte died 1913.
- Content: heading "ROW, ROW. Round.", numbers 1 to 4, the four lines of words. Nothing demeaning.
- Cropped out: the end of "Lightly Row. Concluded." above (one stray fermata of its last bar sits just above the crop's
  top edge and is not in it) and "God Speed the Right", "German", below. Neither is part of this song.

  by Google" mark. Dropped.
- Pammelia (1609) title page: already used on hey-ho-nobody-home.
- Photographs of Lyte: no people in photographs.
- The Teachers' Institute Glee Book title page: carries W. B. Hall's name and the preparers' text; Hall's death year
  not found. Not used.
