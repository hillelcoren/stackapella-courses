# Asleep in the Deep: licence evidence for each image

Gathered 2026-11-13. All three images are pages of one copy: the F. A. Mills bass edition in D, "Copyright, 1897, by
Julie C. Petrie", New York Public Library, Music Division, American popular songs, item
https://digitalcollections.nypl.org/items/d2f26e90-c591-012f-9136-58d385a7bc34 (the story's [^nypl1897]).

The NYPL site answered with a bot check (Incapsula) to plain requests and to WebFetch, and its Wayback copy holds only
that bot page, so NYPL's own rights line was not seen. The scans were taken from Wikimedia Commons, where Fæ's NYPL batch
upload names the holder, the NYPL image ID and the NYPL source link on each file page. Saved beside this file:
raw/commons-info.json (API metadata for all candidates) and raw/commons-464373-1710665.wikitext (the file page source).

## Shared reasoning (PD-old)

- Words: Arthur J. Lamb, 1870 to 1928. Music: Henry W. Petrie, 1857 to 1925 (LC Name Authority n88144360,
  "Petrie, H. W. (Henry W.), 1857-1925"; ASCAP 1948 for both).
- Published in New York with the notice "Copyright, 1897, by Julie C. Petrie"; this undated printing carries Mills's
  48 West 29th St. address (about 1900).
- US: published before 1931, so public domain.
- EU and Israel (life + 70): Petrie's term ended 31 December 1995, Lamb's 31 December 1998. The cover design is
  unsigned; as an anonymous work its term (70 years from publication) ended by 1970.
- The scans are faithful photographs of flat pages and add no new copyright.
- Commons template on each: {{PD-scan|PD-1923|deathyear=1897}}. "deathyear=1897" is a batch-upload placeholder (the
  publication year), not anyone's death year; our own reasoning above does not rely on it. The licence is not a
  "PD in the US only" tag, and our EU/Israel reasoning stands on the writers' death years.
