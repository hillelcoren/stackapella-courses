# Shema Yisrael: image rights evidence

Checked 2026-11-18. Originals in raw/; finals made with sips only (BMP round trip strips metadata; crops with
sips -c and --cropOffset on the BMP, then -Z to JPEG, never upscaled). Every original and both final crops were
opened and looked at; zoomed checks of the inscriptions and the ark are in check/.

Jewish-page rule: no full divine name in any final image, in Hebrew or in transliteration. Both originals show the
Stadttempel's frieze over the ark inscribed with Psalm 16:8 ("I have set Hashem always before me"; written here
with ה' for the Name) with the four-letter name spelled in full and legible at full size (check/litho-frieze-left-out.png,
check/drawing-frieze-left-out.png). The lithograph's tablets above it carry the opening words of the commandments
(check/litho-tablets-left-out.png). Both finals are therefore cropped to the lower part of the hall, starting about
30 px (lithograph) and 40 px (drawing) of the full scans below the bottom of the inscription's letters, so neither the
frieze nor the tablets nor the dome are in them. What is left holds no lettering in any script: the ark's arch has
square rosettes and a carved fan, its door a plain panel (drawing) or an oval ornament (lithograph), both checked at
2x to 3x (check/drawing-ark.png, check/litho-ark.png). No people anywhere in either original.

## 1.jpg (card): Stadttempel interior, drawing, about 1825 to 1826

- Item: "Innenansicht der Synagoge (Stadttempel), 1., Seitenstettengasse 4", Wien Museum, Inventarnummer 65134.
  Künstler: vermutlich (probably) Ernst Ehrenhauss (1797 to 1888), Zeichner; Josef Georg Kornhäusel (1783 to 1860), Architekt.
  Objektart: Grafik, Architektur Handzeichnungen. Datierung: um 1825 to 1826. Technik: Pastellkreidezeichnung,
  laviert, Federzeichnung, on paper; Blattmaß 34.2 x 44.5 cm. Bildmotive: Stadttempel.
  - Record: https://sammlung.wienmuseum.at/objekt/137424-innenansicht-der-synagoge-stadttempel-1-seitenstettengasse-4/
    (English: https://sammlung.wienmuseum.at/en/object/137424-innenansicht-der-synagoge-stadttempel-1-seitenstettengasse-4/)
- Rights statement on the record: badge "CC0", and "Sie können diese Objektabbildung unter den Bedingungen von CC0
  herunterladen und verwenden (teilen, bearbeiten, nutzen, auch kommerziell). Wir bitten Sie, Urheber:in und
  Fotograf:in sowie das Wien Museum als Quelle anzugeben". The record names no photographer; credit gives the
  draughtsman and the museum.
- Public domain by age, as a check: drawn about 1825 to 1826; Ehrenhauss died 1888, Kornhäusel 1860. EU (Austria,
  life plus 70) and Israel (life plus 70): PD since 1959 at the latest. US: an unpublished work whose author died more
  than 70 years ago is PD (17 USC 303), and if it counts as published it is long before 1931. A faithful scan of a
  flat work adds no new right (Bridgeman v. Corel; Art. 14 DSM Directive).
- Download: https://sammlung.wienmuseum.at/images/objects/137424/543268_full.jpg (3500 x 2503, with a colour chart at
  the left; EXIF "Wien Museum, Inventarnummer HMW 65134"), saved as raw/wm-65134-drawing.jpg.
- Crop: BMP crop 2350 x 1240 at x 600, y 1150 of the full scan, -Z 1800, JPEG quality 82. Final 1800 x 949,
  576,457 bytes. Left out: the frieze inscription with the name, the tablets (the drawing marks only a few strokes on
  the right tablet), the oval dome and its ring of rosettes, the colour chart, the paper margins and the sheet number
  at top right. Kept: the aisle, the pews and screens, the ark under its arch with the hanging lamp, the two
  candlesticks, both chandeliers, the balustrades and the lower gallery; a fold line in the paper runs faintly
  through the floor. The small mark at the foot of the right screen is a drawn latch, not a letter (checked at 3x).
- Caption facts: "drawn around its opening in 1826" from the record's date (about 1825 to 1826) and the brief's
  fact that the Stadttempel opened in 1826; Sulzer its cantor from 1826 from the brief.

## 2.jpg: Stadttempel interior, lithograph by Ernst Ehrenhauss after Kornhäusel, about 1826

- Item: "Synagoge in der Seitenstettengasse 4 (Innenansicht), C. N. 494", Wien Museum, Inventarnummer 14710.
  Ernst Ehrenhauss (1797 to 1888), Lithograf; nach: Josef Georg Kornhäusel (1783 to 1860), Zeichner. Lithografie on
  paper, "um 1826"; Blattmaß 45.7 x 53.4 cm, Bildmaß 36 x 47 cm. Shown in "Wien. Meine Geschichte" (Biedermeier und
  Vormärz). Inscription as recorded: li. u. "Ehrenhauss del. lithogr.", re. u. "ged. im lithogr. Institut in Wien";
  below: "Innere Ansicht des Israelitischen Tempels zu Wien erbaut unter der glorreichen (sic) Regierung Seiner K.K.
  Majestät Franz I./ von den israelitischen Bewohnern Wien's unter der Leitung des Herrn Architecten
  Kornhäusel/eingeweihet zum Gottesdienste am 9ten April 1826".
  - Record: https://sammlung.wienmuseum.at/objekt/78123-synagoge-in-der-seitenstettengasse-4-innenansicht-c-n-494/
    (English: https://sammlung.wienmuseum.at/en/object/78123-synagoge-in-der-seitenstettengasse-4-innenansicht-c-n-494)
- Rights statement on the record: badge "CC0", with the same CC0 wording as above. (The record prints a dash before "auch" and in its date ranges; quotes here write it as a comma or "to".)
- Public domain by age, as a check: published about 1826 (the print's own text dates the consecration 9 April 1826);
  Ehrenhauss died 1888, Kornhäusel 1860. US: published before 1931, PD. EU and Israel: life plus 70 long expired.
- Download: https://sammlung.wienmuseum.at/images/objects/78123/498168_full.jpg (3500 x 2813, with colour charts and
  rulers at the edges; EXIF "Wien Museum, Inventarnummer HMW 14710"), saved as raw/wm-14710-litho.jpg.
- Crop: BMP crop 2540 x 1180 at x 560, y 1150 of the full scan, -Z 1600, JPEG quality 70 (82 and 75 gave 646 KB and
  601 KB; the litho grain compresses poorly). Final 1600 x 743, 558,414 bytes. Left out: the frieze inscription, the
  tablets, the dome, the printed frame, the signature lines and the printed title (which repeats no divine name but
  is a page of text), the pencil numbers "2034" and "263" below it, and the colour charts. Kept: the aisle, screens,
  pews, the reading desk behind its railing, the ark door under its arch, candlesticks, chandeliers, gallery and the
  curving staircase on the right.
- Caption facts: the consecration date from the print's own title (as transcribed on the record); "the year Sulzer
  began as its cantor" from the brief.
