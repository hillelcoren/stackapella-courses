## 3.jpg: Singer, Authorised Daily Prayer Book, 9th ed. (London, 1912), Hebrew p. 171

- Item: "The Authorised daily prayer book of the United Hebrew Congregations of the British Empire : with a new
  translation / by S. Singer ; published under the sanction of the late chief rabbi Dr. Nathan Marcus Adler." 9th
  ed. London : Eyre and Spottiswood, 1912. ix, 329, 329 p. "Hebrew and English on opposite pages, numbered in
  duplicate." (MARC: https://archive.org/download/theauthoriseddai00unknuoft/theauthoriseddai00unknuoft_marc.xml)
- Holder: University of Toronto, Robarts Library, call number ARJ-7398; digitized by the Internet Archive.
  Item: https://archive.org/details/theauthoriseddai00unknuoft
- Rights statement: IA metadata (https://archive.org/metadata/theauthoriseddai00unknuoft):
  `"possible-copyright-status": "NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT"`, `"contributor": "Robarts - University of Toronto"`.
- Public domain by age:
  - Published London 1912. Simeon Singer (translator, 1848-1906) and Nathan Marcus Adler (1803-1890) per the
    record. The Hebrew text is the medieval hymn. Even if the 9th edition was revised by Israel Abrahams (died
    1925), every possible author died before 1956.
  - US: published 1912, before 1931, PD. EU and Israel (life plus 70): PD since 1977 for Singer, 1996 at the latest
    for any reviser. UK typographical arrangement right (25 years): long expired.
  - A faithful scan of a flat page adds no new right (Bridgeman; Art. 14 DSM).
- Download: https://archive.org/download/theauthoriseddai00unknuoft/page/n322.jpg (1852 x 3028), saved as
  raw/ia-theauthoriseddai00unknuoft-n322.jpg. Leaf n322 is the Hebrew p. 171. The final keeps the page head and
  Adon Olam down to the rule under it; it leaves out the next piece (שיר היחוד ליום השבת and its rubric).
- What it shows, every word read: header "מוסף לשבת ויום טוב", page number "171", and
  the ten lines of Adon Olam from "אדון עולם אשר מלך" to "יי לי ולא אירא". The divine name in the last line is
  printed as the customary two-yod abbreviation. Faint show-through from the back of the leaf is the English of the
  Mourner's Kaddish ("Magnified and sanctified be his great name...", "And now, I pray thee, let the power of the
  Lord be great..."): liturgy, nothing demeaning. No ornament, no figure.
- Caption facts: the leaf before (English p. 170, n323) ends the Additional Service with the end of Alenu and the
  Mourner's Kaddish; the facing English p. 171 (n321) is headed "ADDITIONAL SERVICE FOR SABBATHS AND FESTIVALS" and
  gives Adon Olam in English; nothing of the service follows Adon Olam except an optional hymn "In some
  Congregations ... said before 'Blessed be he who spake'". The story: "its main place is at the close of the
  Musaf" ([^ej]) and Ashkenazi books print ten lines ([^je]). Abrahams's Companion ([^abrahams]) is the companion
  to this very prayer book.
