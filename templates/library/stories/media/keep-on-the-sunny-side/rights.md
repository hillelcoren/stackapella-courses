## Rejected

- Heavenly Sunlight No. 40 (image n43): clean and public domain (Blenkhorn, Entwisle only; "Copyright, MDCCCXCIX,
  by J. Howard Entwisle."), but the same engraving as 2.jpg; one music page is enough.
- No photograph found worth adding: the Carter Family places (Maces Spring, Camden, Del Rio) would need a free
  photographer's licence and nothing was checked; none used.
- Listen: no recording of this song before 1926 exists on the National Jukebox or the Internet Archive (searched by
  title and by Blenkhorn and Entwisle). The 1906 Victor and Columbia "Keep on the sunny side" are Morse and
  Drislane's different song; the 1925 "On the Sunny Side of Life" fox trot is another; the 1928 Carter Family
  record is not public domain everywhere and is never linked.

- Lead, 2026-11-13: 3.jpg (Heavenly Sunlight cover, 1900) dropped: co-editor Powell G. Fithian (born 1861) has no known death year, so its term can not be shown to have ended everywhere.
