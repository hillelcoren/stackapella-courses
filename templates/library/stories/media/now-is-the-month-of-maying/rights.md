trip strips metadata), cropped, never enlarged. Every printed word on each final was read (listed below); nothing

Makers' death years (Mexico life plus 100 checked too): Thomas Morley, composer and author, died 1602 or soon after
(the song file: 1602; the story: 1602 to 1604); the English words are anonymous (1595); the Italian words of 3.jpg
are from Orazio Vecchi, died 1605; printers Thomas Este (East), died about 1608, and Peter Short, died 1603. No
modern editor's work appears on any final. All are public domain everywhere: US (published before 1931), UK/EU and
Israel (life plus 70: expired by about 1680), Mexico (life plus 100: expired by about 1710). A flat microfilm scan of
