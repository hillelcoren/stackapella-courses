# Noche de paz: image rights evidence

Checked 2026-11-13. Originals in raw/ (downloaded with plain curl from archive.org); finals made with sips only (BMP
round trip strips metadata; `sips -g all` shows no EXIF). Silent Night's own set is not reused.

All four are scans of flat printed pages from three Princeton Theological Seminary Library books, digitized by the
Internet Archive (IA metadata "contributor": Princeton Theological Seminary Library; "sponsor": Calvin College). The IA
records state no licence, so each rests on public domain by age, reasoned below. A faithful scan of a flat page adds
no new copyright: in the US under Bridgeman v. Corel, and in the EU under Art. 14 of the 2019 DSM Directive and for
lack of originality. No people are drawn or photographed on any of them; no logos; no watermarks.

## 1.jpg and 2.jpg: El Himnario evangélico (New York, 1893)

- Item: El Himnario evangélico : para el uso de todas las iglesias. Nueva York : Sociedad Americana de Tratados,
  1893. 1 close score (240 p.), 22 cm. Call number SCC. OCLC 1044652380. IA item elhimevangl93amer
  (https://archive.org/details/elhimevangl93amer), ark:/13960/t9p27zv13, scanned 2012.
- 1.jpg: title page, IA image n6, https://archive.org/download/elhimevangl93amer/page/n6.jpg (2118 x 3118), saved as
  raw/ia-himnario-1893-title.jpg; the final is the whole page, resized. The page reads "EL HIMNARIO EVANGÉLICO. PARA
  EL USO DE TODAS LAS IGLESIAS." and "SOCIEDAD AMERICANA DE TRATADOS, 150 CALLE DE NASSAU, NUEVA YORK." The centre
  vignette (inspected at full size) is a still life only: a lyre, rolled scrolls, a horn, a jug, papers and smoke or
  cloud. No figure or face.
- 2.jpg: page 38, IA image n43, https://archive.org/download/elhimevangl93amer/page/n43.jpg (2189 x 3125), saved as
  raw/ia-himnario-1893-p38.jpg; the final crops to the top 1885 px: No. 48 in full (heading, music, three verses) and
  the heading and first system of No. 49, "Los heraldos celestiales", tune "FABEN.", "J. H. WILCOX." Every word on
  the crop read: running head "EL SEÑOR JESU-CRISTO: SU ENCARNACION.", the No. 48 words, No. 49's title and first
  line "Los heraldos celestiales Cantan con sonora voz: ¡Gloria al Rey recién na-". Nothing that demeans anyone.
- Publication: the full text (https://archive.org/stream/elhimevangl93amer/elhimevangl93amer_djvu.txt) has
  "150 CALLE DE NASSAU, NUEVA YORK." and "COPYRIGHT, 1893." The title page and preface name no editors (story,
  [^h1893-preface]); No. 48 is unsigned and names no composer.
- People involved and their deaths: Gruber (tune) 1863; Mohr (German original) 1848; the Spanish words unsigned, often
  credited to Federico Fliedner, died 1901; J. H. Wilcox (No. 49 tune; John Henry Wilcox) 1875; American Tract Society
  as publisher.
- US: published 1893, before 1931: PD.
- EU: an anonymous work's term is 70 years from publication (Term Directive art. 1(3)): ended 1963. Even if the
  words are Fliedner's, life plus 70 ended 1971; every named or likely author died by 1916 (J. B. Cabrera, signed
  elsewhere in the book, died 1916: ended 1986).
- Israel: life plus 70, or 70 years from publication for anonymous works: long expired.

## 3.jpg: Himnario provisional (Philadelphia, 1907), No. 18

- Item: Himnario provisional con los cánticos según el uso della iglesia Episcopal Americana : para Congregaciones
  Españoles. Philadelphia : John C. Winston Co., 1907. Creator (IA): Van Buren, James Heartt, 1850-1917. Call number
  SCC. OCLC 1045997205. IA item himnari00vanb (https://archive.org/details/himnari00vanb).
- Page: IA image n24, https://archive.org/download/himnari00vanb/page/n24.jpg (2607 x 3924), saved as
  raw/ia-himnario-provisional-1907-no18.jpg. The final crops from y 1505 to the foot: No. 18 only, from "Navidad."
  down. It cuts away the hymn above it and its credit line "SANTIAGO PASCOE. (Tr.)", so nothing on the crop credits
  anyone. Words on the crop: "Navidad.", "18.", "8. 7. 10. 10. 7. 7.", "NOCHE BUENA.", "A-men.", and the three verses.
- Publication: the full text (https://archive.org/stream/himnari00vanb/himnari00vanb_djvu.txt) reads
  "Obispo de Puerto Rico.", "THE JOHN C WINSTON COMPANY, ... 1907.", "In the year 1907.", and the Aviso is dated
  May 1907.
- US: published 1907: PD. EU and Israel: Van Buren died 1917, life plus 70 ended 1987; the words are unsigned
  (70 years from 1907); Gruber died 1863.

## 4.jpg: El Nuevo himnario evangélico (New York, 1915), No. 83

- Item: El Nuevo himnario evangélico : para el uso de las iglesias evangélicas de habla española en todo el mundo,
  words edition ("EDICIÓN SIN MÚSICA"). New York : Sociedad Americana de Tratados. Call number SCB. OCLC 1044587290.
  IA item elnuev00newy (https://archive.org/details/elnuev00newy).
- Page 32: IA image n51, https://archive.org/download/elnuev00newy/page/n51.jpg (2692 x 3180), saved as
  raw/ia-nuevo-himnario-1915-p32.jpg. The final crops the top of the right-hand column only: the last three lines of
  verse 1 and verses 2 and 3 of No. 83. Nothing of Nos. 82 or 84 is on the crop. (No. 83's heading and first two
  lines sit at the foot of the left column and are not included.)
- Publication: full text (https://archive.org/stream/elnuev00newy/elnuev00newy_djvu.txt): "SOCIEDAD AMERICANA DE
  TRATADOS / PARK AVENUE AND FORTIETH STREET, NEW YORK / COPYRIGHT, 1915, By AMERICAN TRACT SOCIETY". The publisher's
  note names the fifteen-member committee that prepared the hymnal; No. 83 itself is unsigned.
- US: published 1915, before 1931: PD.
- EU and Israel: the words are the anonymous 1893 text with one line and two phrases of verse 2 changed by an unnamed reviser; the work itself
  attributes the hymn to no one, so it is an anonymous work: 70 years from publication, ended 1985. The changes are
  also too slight to be a new work. For the checker: this is the one image whose EU reasoning rests on the anonymous-
  work rule rather than on known death dates; the committee's members are named for the book as a whole, not for
  this hymn.

## Rejected or not used

- Silent Night's images (Oberndorf flood slide, Hollister 1863): kept to that song, as briefed.
- The full 1915 page: its other hymns (No. 82, a translation of Emily Elliott's hymn, and No. 84) have translators
  unnamed on the page whose dates we could not check (Hymnary.org refused plain requests); cropped out instead.
- The 1907 page's upper hymn and its "Santiago Pascoe (Tr.)" credit: cropped out, as it would read as a credit for
  No. 18.
- Himnos evangélicos (1894, Library of Congress copy): words only, much like 4.jpg; not needed.
- No public-domain photograph of Federico Fliedner was sought: the story says his authorship is a credit, not a proof,
  and a portrait would imply more.
