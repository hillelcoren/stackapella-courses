# White Coral Bells: image rights evidence

Checked 2026-11-13. Originals in raw/. Finals made with sips only (BMP round trip strips metadata), scaled to 1600 px
on the long side. A faithful scan or photograph of a flat public-domain work adds no new copyright: in the US under
Bridgeman v. Corel, in the EU under Art. 14 of the 2019 DSM Directive, and in Israel for lack of originality. Every
image looked at in full and every printed word on each crop read; none shows a drawn, painted or engraved human
figure, a watermark, or a logo (the publisher's device on the title page is cropped out). No photographs in the set.
Total 4 files, about 1.1 MB (172 KB, 237 KB, 561 KB, 220 KB).

Makers check (Mexico, life plus 100): the song's only listed maker is Hollis Dann (edition; died 1939, song file
`makers`). Images 1, 2 and 4 are Dann's own book: his title page, his preface, and the anonymous round he printed, so
they ride with the song page. No other named person's words or music is on those crops: "Busy Wheels" (above the
round on p. 115) and the "Two-Part Study" (below) are cut away. Image 3 is a 1772 print: Magdalena Bouchard (active
in Rome in the 1770s) and Giorgio Bonelli (Hortus Romanus, died 1782) are far past life plus 100.

Not used: the 1943 Alabama 4-H songbook (institutional work of 1943, within 100 years in Mexico); the 1947 Hymns of the
Rural Spirit (protected); Grieve's Modern Herbal (1931); the 1907 Garden Month by Month (authors' death years not
established). Reuse check: no media.json under stories/media/ or state/media/ cites Dann's Fourth Year Music, Wellcome
16581i/V0042710EL, Bouchard, or any lily of the valley (grep "lily|lilies|convallaria|hollisdann|Fourth Year
Music|Bouchard").
