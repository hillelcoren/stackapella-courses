- Item record (archive.org metadata): date "1855"; publisher "Dublin : Printed at the University Press for the Society for the Preservation and Publication of the Melodies of Ireland by M. H. Gill"; creator "Petrie, George, 1790-1866, ed. and arr". The record has no rights field; the notes say "No copyright page found."
- Public domain by age: published 1855. Editor and arranger George Petrie died 1866; the record names no other author.
  - US: published before 1931, so public domain.
  - EU (Ireland): life plus 70, ended 31 December 1936.
  - Israel: life plus 70, ended 1936.
- A scan of a flat public-domain page: no new copyright in the photograph.
- The page shows what our story quotes: the heading "Name unknown", the thanks to "Miss J. Ross, of N.-T.-Limavady, in the county of Londonderry", and "The name of the tune unfortunately was not ascertained by Miss Ross".

## 2.jpg: Danny Boy, Boosey & Co., 1913, cover

- Holder: Greer Music Library, Connecticut College, Historic Sheet Music Collection 45: https://digitalcommons.conncoll.edu/sheetmusic/45 (record: Publication Date 1913; "Sung by Mme. Schumann-Heink, Miss Florence Hinkle and Mr. Dan Beddoe; Adapted from an Old Irish Air by Fred. E. Weatherly; Price 60 cents; Boosey & Co. 9 East Seventeenth Street, New York and 295 Regent Streent, London, Eng.; This song may be sung in public without fee or license the public performance of any parodied version, however, is strictly prohibited"). The record gives no rights statement.
- The college's PDF download is behind a browser check, so the image was taken from the Internet Archive mirror of the same PDF, whose record names the college page as its source: https://archive.org/details/sm_dannyboy (description: "Source: digitalcommons.conncoll.edu/sheetmusic/45"). Image n1, downloaded from https://archive.org/download/sm_dannyboy/page/n1.jpg (3684 x 4896). Image n0 is the college's cover sheet and was not used.
- The cover reads "COPYRIGHT MCMXIII BY BOOSEY & CO." and "PRINTED IN U.S.A.": this is the 1913 printing (60 cents, Boosey & Co., 9 East Seventeenth Street), not a later Boosey & Hawkes reprint.
- Public domain by age: published 1913.
  - US: published before 1931, so public domain.
  - Words and adaptation: Frederic E. Weatherly died 1929. EU and Israel: life plus 70, ended 31 December 1999.
  - The cover lettering and the piano part's arranger are not named, so they count as anonymous: 70 years from publication, ended 1983 in the EU and Israel.
- The cover is all type and small ornaments. There are no figures. It has a few handwritten notes by a past owner ("Souls of the righteous / Noble" in pencil and "Boosey & Co" in ink).
- Rejected alternatives: University of Alberta SM000968 (https://archive.org/details/SM000968, "Deemed public domain") and BYU dannyboysong00weat are later Boosey & Hawkes printings, so they are not the 1913 printing. Mississippi State's Templeton copy (scholarsjunction.msstate.edu/cht-sheet-music/12024) says "Item Unavailable Due to Copyright Restrictions", so it was not used.
