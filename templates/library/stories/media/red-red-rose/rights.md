# A Red, Red Rose: rights evidence for each image

Checked 2026-11-13. Every image was opened and looked at, page edges and feet zoomed: no drawn, painted or engraved
human figures, no vignettes, no photographs, no logos. Every printed word on all four pages was read: song words,
tempo marks, instrument names, page and song numbers, and headings. Nothing mocks or demeans any people or faith.
None of these items is used on another song page (checked every stories/media/*/media.json; the 1839 Museum vol. 5
scan is also the source of pages for Auld Lang Syne and Comin' Thro' the Rye, but a different page, p. 415, is used
here).

Not hosted, on purpose: the National Library of Scotland scans the story cites for the Museum (Ing.43,
scotsmusicalmuv300john; 4453630, scotsmusicalmuse01rugg) and for Smith (4454074, scotishminstrels00smit). Their item
records carry licenseurl http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/2.5/scotland/ and "National Library of
Scotland holds full rights in this digital resource and agrees to license the resource under the Creative Commons
License: Attribution-Noncommercial-Share Alike". NC and SA are not allowed, so other holders' copies of the same
pages are used.

Makers and life plus 100 (Mexico): every maker on every page died by 1878 (Urbani 1816, Johnson 1811, Clarke 1797,
Burns 1796, Smith 1829; 1839 editors Stenhouse 1827, Sharpe 1851, Laing 1878), so each page is free even under life
plus 100 (the latest ran out at the end of 1978). No page carries text or music by anyone still protected anywhere.
