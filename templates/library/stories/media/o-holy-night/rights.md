## Lead's ruling on 1.jpg (2026-11-12)
Kept. The holder (Bayerische Staatsbibliothek) marks its own scan Public Domain Mark 1.0 in its IIIF manifest; the
Deutsche Digitale Bibliothek's CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 is the aggregator's record, not the holder's. The work (Schott cover,
about 1852; Adam d. 1856, Cappeau d. 1877) is long out of copyright, and a faithful scan of a two-dimensional
public-domain work gains no new right: EU Directive 2019/790 Art. 14, in German law UrhG §68 (since 7 June 2021);
in the US, Bridgeman v. Corel (1999). The page has lettering and scrollwork only, no figure.
