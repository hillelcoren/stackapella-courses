# Joy to the World: image rights evidence

Checked 2026-11-12. Originals in raw/; finals made with sips only (BMP round trip strips metadata), resized to 1600 px
on the long side, nothing cropped.

The three page images in raw/ (watts_n288.jpg, op_n71.jpg, np_n135.jpg) came from an earlier run whose notes were
lost. I re-downloaded each from the Internet Archive URL given below and checked that it is byte-identical (MD5
40acbc9c..., de3340ba..., d0e79186...), so each raw file is exactly the holder's scan of the page named. The *_meta.json,
*_pages.json and op_djvu.txt files in raw/ are left from that run and were not used; I fetched the records again.

None of the three Internet Archive records carries a licence statement (no "rights" or "licenseurl" field), so each
rests on public domain by age, with the reasoning below. Each is a faithful scan of a flat printed page, which adds no
new copyright: in the US under Bridgeman v. Corel, and in the EU under Art. 14 of the 2019 DSM Directive and for lack of
originality; Israel likewise requires originality.

## 1.jpg: Watts, The Psalms of David Imitated (London, 1719), p. 253

- Item: "The Psalms of David : imitated in the language of the New Testament, and apply'd to the Christian state and
  worship", London: Printed for J. Clark ... R. Ford ... and R. Cruttenden, 1719. Creator "Watts, Isaac, 1674-1748".
  Contributor and sponsor: Princeton Theological Seminary Library; call number SCB. The record notes copy 1 carries a
  handwritten inscription "To my Hon. Uncle Mr. Richard Taunton, Jan. 15th, 1718/9. I. W.".
- Record: https://archive.org/details/chrtwimi00watt (metadata https://archive.org/metadata/chrtwimi00watt).
  Page: https://archive.org/details/chrtwimi00watt/page/n288/mode/1up ; file
  https://archive.org/download/chrtwimi00watt/page/n288.jpg (1640 x 2880), saved as raw/watts_n288.jpg.
- PD by age: published 1719; Watts died 1748. US: published before 1931. EU and Israel: life plus 70 ended in 1818.
- What it shows: type only, page 253, the end of Psalm 98 first part, Watts's italic note, then "Psalm XCVIII. Second
  Part. The Messiah's Coming and Kingdom" with all four verses. No figures, ornaments with people, logos or
  watermarks.
- Caption facts: the heading and page are on the image. "Earliest known printing" is the story's own
  earliestPrinting (watts1719) and its opening ("printed in London in 1719" [^watts1719][^canterbury]). The italic note
  ("In these two Hymns which I have formed out of the 98th Psalm I have fully exprest what I esteem to be the first
  and chief Sense ...") is quoted in the story's "The words" section.

## 2.jpg: Mason, Occasional Psalm and Hymn Tunes (Boston, 1836), No. 73, p. 70

- Item: "Occasional psalm and hymn tunes : selected and original", Boston: For sale by J. H. Wilkins & R. B. Carter.
  Creator "Mason, Lowell, 1792-1872". Contributor and sponsor: Boston Public Library; call number BRLL; OCLC 25335787.
  IA dates the bound copy 1837; the OCR of the book's own notice reads "Entered according to Act of Congress, in the
  year 1836, by Melvin Lord, in the Clerk's Office of the District Court of Massachusetts"
  (https://archive.org/download/occasionalpsalmh00maso/occasionalpsalmh00maso_djvu.txt). The story gives the same
  dating ([^mason1836]).
- Record: https://archive.org/details/occasionalpsalmh00maso . Page:
  https://archive.org/details/occasionalpsalmh00maso/page/n71/mode/1up ; file
  https://archive.org/download/occasionalpsalmh00maso/page/n71.jpg (3003 x 1839), saved as raw/op_n71.jpg.
- PD by age: published 1836 or 1837; Mason died 1872 (the tune's English source is anonymous and older). US: before
  1931. EU and Israel: life plus 70 ended in 1942.
- What it shows: printed music and words only: "70 No. 73. ANTIOCH. C. M. Arranged from Handel. Ch. Ps. Ps. 98, 2d
  pt.", four staves, verses 2 to 4 at the right. No figures, logos or watermarks.
- Caption facts: number, title, "Arranged from Handel" and "Ps. 98, 2d pt." are on the page. The story: "In 1836 he
  printed the tune as No. 73 ... named it ANTIOCH, marked it 'Arranged from Handel' and set it to Watts's Psalm 98,
  second part" [^mason1836][^hymnarchive].

## 3.jpg: Mason and Webb, The National Psalmist (Boston, 1848), p. 132

- Item: "The national psalmist", Boston: Tappan, Whittemore, and Mason, 1848. Creators "Mason, Lowell, 1792-1872,
  compiler" and "Webb, George James, 1803-1887". Contributor and sponsor: Boston Public Library; call number BRLL;
  OCLC 1061471.
- Record: https://archive.org/details/nationalpsalmist00maso . Page:
  https://archive.org/details/nationalpsalmist00maso/page/n135/mode/1up ; file
  https://archive.org/download/nationalpsalmist00maso/page/n135.jpg (3211 x 1949), saved as raw/np_n135.jpg.
- PD by age: published 1848; Mason died 1872, Webb 1887. US: before 1931. EU and Israel: life plus 70 ended in 1957.
- What it shows: printed music and words only: "132 ANTIOCH. C. M.* Arranged from HANDEL.", the tune WARREN at the
  lower right, and the footnote "*This tune is here republished from 'Carmina Sacra,' though, as is believed, in an
  improved form. It has been very popular for Choir and Singing School practice, to which it should, perhaps, be
  principally confined ...". Faint show-through from the next page. No figures, logos or watermarks.
- Caption facts: page and footnote on the image; the story says "Our arrangement reads Mason's four parts from The
  National Psalmist of 1848, page 132" and quotes the footnote [^mason1848].

- Mason's Modern Psalmist (1839), p. 144 (the D-major printing): not fetched; a third ANTIOCH page would repeat 2.jpg
