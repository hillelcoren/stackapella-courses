# O Little Town of Bethlehem: image rights evidence

Checked 2026-11-13. Originals in raw/; finals made with sips only (BMP round trip strips metadata; crops done on the
BMP with sips -c and --cropOffset), long side 1600 px. Sizes: 1.jpg 353 KB, 2.jpg 526 KB (quality 64), 3.jpg 424 KB,

The two book pages rest on public domain by age: neither Internet Archive record carries a licence field ("rights",
"licenseurl" and "possible-copyright-status" are empty). Each is a faithful scan of a flat printed page, which adds no
new copyright: in the US under Bridgeman v. Corel, in the EU under Art. 14 of the 2019 DSM Directive and for lack of
originality; Israel likewise requires originality.

## 1.jpg: Phillips Brooks, cabinet photograph by Elliott & Fry, about 1887 to 1892

- Item: "The late Lord Bishop of Massachusetts, the right Rev. Phillips Brooks, D. D." Genre: Photographs; Cabinet
  photographs; Portrait photographs. Date "[ca. 1887–1892]" (dateCreated; "Date supplied by cataloger."). Names:
  "Elliott & Fry" as Photographer and as Copyright holder; statement of responsibility "Elliott & Fry, photographers,
  55, Baker Street, London. W." Extent: "1 photograph : print on card mount ; mount 17 x 11 cm (cabinet card
  format)". Local id 22_07_001770. Subject: Brooks, Phillips, 1835-1893.
- Holder: Boston Public Library, Cabinet Card Collection. Record: https://www.digitalcommonwealth.org/search/commonwealth:8c97pm356
  (JSON: https://www.digitalcommonwealth.org/search/commonwealth:8c97pm356.json); permalink
  https://ark.digitalcommonwealth.org/ark:/50959/8c97pm356 .
- Rights statement, exact words on the record: rights "No known copyright restrictions."; license "No known
  restrictions on use."; rightsstatement "No Copyright - United States"
  (http://rightsstatements.org/vocab/NoC-US/1.0/).
- File: the holder's IIIF full image,
  https://iiif.digitalcommonwealth.org/iiif/2/commonwealth:dj530197r/full/full/0/default.jpg (3063 x 4567), saved as
  raw/bpl_elliottfry_brooks.jpg. Final cropped only to the card's edges (scan background removed).
- It is a photograph, not an engraving: a continuous-tone print on a card mount, catalogued as a photograph. One
  person, Brooks himself (died 1893). No drawn figures.
- Public domain reasoning (the NoC-US statement covers the US only, so the rest is ours):
  - US: a commercial cabinet card, issued with the photographers' imprint; the card's wording "The Late" shows it was
    printed after Brooks's death on 23 January 1893. Published before 1931: PD.
  - EU and UK: the firm's founders were Joseph John Elliott (1835 to 1903) and Clarence Edmund Fry (1840 to 1897);
    their partnership was dissolved in 1887 and Elliott carried on the Baker Street studio under the old name
    (photohistory-sussex.co.uk, "Fry Clarence"; Wikipedia, "Elliott & Fry"). If Elliott took it, life plus 70 ended
    in 1973. If an unnamed studio operator took it, the work is anonymous (credited only to the firm): 70 years from
    publication, ended by 1963 at the latest.
  - Israel: life plus 70, or for an anonymous work 70 years from publication; either way ended long ago.
- Every word on the card: "The Late Lord Bishop of Massachusetts, The Right Rev. Phillips Brooks, D.D." and the
  partly faded studio line "Elliott & Fry ... Copyright ... 55 Baker Street ... London N.W.". A round library stamp
  ("... LIBRARY") at the lower right of the print. The 1890s "Copyright" notice is long expired. Nothing demeaning,
  no modern trademark.
- Caption facts: photographer, place and date from the record. "Bishop of Massachusetts from 1891, died on 23 January
  1893" is the story's own sentence ([^benson1903][^hymnarchive-brooks]).

## 2.jpg: Bethlehem, general view, about 1900 (American Colony, Matson Collection)

- Item: "Bethlehem and surroundings. General view of Bethlehem." Abstract/medium: G. Eric and Edith Matson Photograph
  Collection. Physical description: 1 negative (a stereo pair on one plate). Date: 1900 as given on the Commons
  record; I write "about 1900" since many Matson dates are the cataloguer's. Place: West Bank--Bethlehem.
  Reproduction number LC-DIG-matpc-00970; catalog mpc2004001060.
- Holder: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division. Catalog page:
  https://www.loc.gov/pictures/collection/matpc/item/mpc2004001060/PP ; original
  https://hdl.loc.gov/loc.pnp/matpc.00970 .
- loc.gov answered 403 to every plain request (Cloudflare), lccn.loc.gov answered "No Connections Available", the
  Wayback Machine has no copy of this item page, and its CDX index was "Temporarily Offline" during this run. So the
  item record was read from its Commons transcription:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bethlehem_and_surroundings._General_view_of_Bethlehem._LOC_matpc.00970.jpg
  (wikitext saved in work/commons_00970.wiki): source "Library of Congress", catalog link as above,
  "permission=No known restrictions on publication.", licence tags PD-Matson and PD-old-70-1923, author "Matson
  Collection". **For the checker: please confirm the LoC item page's Rights Advisory directly if loc.gov lets you in.**
- Collection-level statement (read via the Wayback copy of 2017 in the Lecha Dodi check, IMAGE-CHECK-3,
  state/media/lecha-dodi/rights.md): http://www.loc.gov/rr/print/res/258_mats.html , "Photographs in the G. Eric and
  Edith Matson Photograph Collection are in the public domain. ... In 2003, the Home dedicated the intellectual
  property and related rights to the collection to the American public".
- File: raw/commons-matpc-00970.jpg (6013 x 4398), LoC's master as hosted on Commons,
  https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/42/Bethlehem_and_surroundings._General_view_of_Bethlehem._LOC_matpc.00970.jpg
  (an attempt at LoC's own derivative on tile.loc.gov stalled and was abandoned). The final is the right-hand frame of
  the stereo pair, cropped from the top of the sky to the plate's foot: sips -c 3100 2700 --cropOffset 1150 3060, so
  the plate's edges, the centre join and the emulsion marks at the top right are gone. No words on the image.
- Public domain reasoning, beside the LoC statement (same as Lecha Dodi's Safed view):
  - US: made about 1900 by the American Colony Photo Department, which sold its prints and stereographs commercially;
    published before 1931: PD.
  - EU: no individual photographer named (corporate credit to the Photo Dept.), so the anonymous-work term applies:
    70 years from being made available, long ended.
  - Israel: made under the old law (Copyright Act 1911 as in force in Mandate Palestine), 50 years from the making of
    the negative; works free before the 2007 law stay free.
  - Caveat as before: if a named individual were ever proved the author, the EU term would follow that person's life.
- What it shows: flat-roofed stone houses and trees in the foreground; terraced gardens with stone walls on the left;
  large stone buildings and a square bell tower along the skyline; a minaret; a cone-shaped hill on the horizon. I see
  no people at this size. Not a photochrom, no drawn figures, no logos or watermark.
- Caption facts: maker and date from the record. Brooks's letter of 30 December 1865 describing "a good-looking town
  on a ridge among terraced gardens" is the story's paraphrase of [^lot1893]. The caption names no building and does
  not claim this is the view Brooks saw.

## Rejected

- Hutchins, Carols Old and Carols New (1916), p. 484, the printing our arrangement is read from: not fetched; it
  would repeat the Church Porch page (same tune and four stanzas, same key), and four images is the limit. It is
  named in links.md as the alternative "original".
- Ohio 1870 p. 83 (n72), with stanzas 4 and 5 including the omitted stanza: good, but a second page of the same book;
  left out for variety.
- Matson matpc.00971 (same stereo view, damaged plate with a light streak and the number "818" on it) and
  matpc.06473 (Bethlehem about 1900, two people in the foreground and a badly flaking emulsion edge).
- Matson Bethlehem views dated "between 1898 and 1914/1946" or 1934 to 1939 (matpc.06759 to 06762, 12198, 04038):
  later or wider dates than 00970.
- Brooks portraits on Commons: book plates of uncertain process (some may be engravings), one CC BY-SA, one sourced
  from a commercial print seller. Other BPL cabinet cards: H. G. Smith (photographer's dates unknown, so the EU term
  cannot be shown), and two group portraits with other men (Notman). Elliott & Fry chosen: solo, named firm whose
  founders' death years are known.
- The 1916 Victor recording on the Internet Archive (loc-jukebox-19923) is a third-party mirror of the National
  Jukebox, not the holder's page; see links.md.
