# Skye Boat Song: rights evidence for each image

Checked 2026-11-13. Every image was opened and looked at. The photograph was zoomed along its cliff top, slope and
shore: rocks only, no people. No drawn, painted or engraved figures on either printed page (Songs of the North p. 18
has no illustration; Blackie p. 239 is type and music only). Every word on both printed pages was read: Jacobite song
words, a note on the escape, Blackie's prose and a book citation. Nothing mocks or demeans any people or faith.

Makers of the song (song file): Harold Boulton, words, died 1935; Anne Campbell Macleod, air, died 1921; Malcolm
Lawson, arrangement, died 1918. The song itself is withheld in Mexico until 2036 (Boulton).

Life plus 100 (Mexico) check for every printed page:
- 1.jpg carries only the makers' own work (Boulton, Macleod, Lawson, and the editors' footnote; the editors are
  Macleod and Boulton). It rides with the song page.
- 2.jpg carries John Stuart Blackie's own prose (died 1895, so free in Mexico since 1996) and the makers' tune and
  words. The footnote only names a book by William Jolly (title, place, year); none of Jolly's text is printed.
