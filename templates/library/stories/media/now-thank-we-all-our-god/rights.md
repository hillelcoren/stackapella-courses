painted or engraved human figure appears on any file; the one photograph (1.jpg) is cropped so that no people are

  Lyra Germanica series: died 1860.
- No page carries text or music by anyone else: 2.jpg is cropped to start at "239." so the end of No. 238

  Commons API LicenseShortName: "CC0".
- So the rights holder, the postcard publisher Brück & Sohn of Meißen, uploaded its own archive copy under CC0 1.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The postcard is also a 1903 photograph of a building.
- Cropped to the church and the printed caption "Rinckardtstrasse."; the street with its small figures, the lamp

- Caption claims: archdeacon from 27 April 1617 to his death 8 December 1649, buried "in der Stadtkirche, wo er
