## 2.jpg: Loudon, Arboretum et Fruticetum Britannicum, vol. 7 (1838), plate LXII. E, p. 207, the weeping willow

- Item: https://archive.org/details/b33491793_0007 (Wellcome Library copy), leaf n100 = plate p. 207 (seen; found by
  the scan's full-text search for "Babylonian"). Downloaded https://archive.org/download/b33491793_0007/page/n100.jpg
  (2261 x 4112).
- Internet Archive record: title "Arboretum et fruticetum Britannicum; or, the trees and shrubs of Britain ...";
  creator "Loudon, J. C. (John Claudius), 1783-1843"; date 1838; volume "Vol. 7"; publisher "London : Printed for the
  author, Longman"; description "Vols. 1-4, text; v. 5-8, plates"; rights "This work is available under the Creative
  Commons, Public Domain Mark" (http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/).
- PD reasoning: printed in London in 1838; Loudon died 1843. The plate carries no artist's or engraver's name; anyone
  drawing or engraving for a book printed in 1838 would have had to live past 100 to reach 1926, so it is public
  domain in the US (published before 1931), the EU and Israel (life plus 70) and Mexico (life plus 100).
- Every printed word on the page: "LXII. E." "207" "Salix babylonica." "The Babylonian, or Weeping, Willow." "19 ft.
  high, 4 in. diam." Nothing else. No people or figures: a tree and botanical details only.
- Caption claim: the story, "What the words mean", first strain: "A willow bends low in the wind and springs back."
  The caption does not say the Shakers meant this species.

## Rejected

- Mary Hazzard's manuscript pages (Winterthur ASC 893): Winterthur not cleared; kept as the "original" link only.
- Andrews, The Gift to Be Simple (1940) and "Shaker Songs" (1937): Andrews died 1964; not public domain.
- Hamilton College's Shaker manuscript hymnals on the Internet Archive. Looked at closely: Elizabeth Garvey's
  New Lebanon book (yhm-spe-sha-ixb-ml-06, 1848-1858), p. 193 "As willows by the water courses", with a small 3 above
  a crossed double bar like Hazzard's; and yhm-spe-sha-ixb-ml-04 (1833-1866), p. 49 "O this beautiful robe of comfort
  & love", "1st Order Jan 30th 1841". Rights field only says to contact the library about "licensing, or copyright
  status"; Hamilton's copyright page (https://litsdigital.hamilton.edu/copyright) gives no statement for them; and the
  writers' death years (Garvey's included) could not be found for the Mexico check. Not clearly OK, as on the two
  sibling pages. Not used.
- Blinn, The Manifestation of Spiritualism among the Shakers (East Canterbury, 1899), p. 51 (Laughing John): HathiTrust
  (full view, "pd") answered only a bot check, no other scan found, and it is a Canterbury item. Not used.
- Isaac N. Youngs, A Short Abridgement of the Rules of Music (New Lebanon, 1843): no scan found online.
- Emerson's Massachusetts trees (1875), "The Weeping Willow ... Mrs. J. Willard": artist's death year unknown. Not used.
- J. P. MacLean, Shakers of Ohio (1907; MacLean died 1939) and Sears, Gleanings from Old Shaker Journals (1916; Sears
  died 1960): not public domain under life plus 100.
- Watervliet South Family "general views" (HABS NY,1-COL,11-7 to 9): the index calls two of them "(unidentified)
  building"; the dwelling (14-3) was chosen instead.
- Any Shaker worship or dance picture: always shows people.
- Canterbury, Enfield (N.H.), Sanbornton, New Lebanon's schoolhouse, Sabbathday Lake, A Sacred Repository, A Summary
  View, A Collection of Millennial Hymns, Millennial Praises and Foster's Canterbury map: on or taken for other pages.
- Only one photograph: one per page at most.
