# Shoshanat Yaakov: image rights evidence

Sourced 2026-11-13. Originals in raw/. Crops cut with Python Pillow into lossless PNGs in work/ (exact pixel boxes
below; sips's own -c/--cropOffset did not land where expected on these files), then every final made with sips only
through a BMP round trip, which strips metadata: `sips -s format bmp work/X.png --out work/tmp.bmp && sips -Z 1600 -s
format jpeg -s formatOptions 82 work/tmp.bmp --out N.jpg` (4.jpg without -Z, so the 993 px scan is not enlarged).

Sizes: 1.jpg 508,720 (1600 x 1190); 2.jpg 319,874 (1176 x 1600); 3.jpg 255,234 (1600 x 688); 4.jpg 84,743 (993 x
234); total 1,168,571 bytes. Trial crops and zoomed checks are in work/.

Photographs: one (1.jpg), with no people in it. Drawn, painted or engraved human figures: none in any final.

## Divine names (batch 8 rule): what each crop keeps and leaves out

Every word in each final was read, the Hebrew in zoomed strips at full scan size.

- **1.jpg keeps** one column of the scroll, Esther 1:19 (end) to 2:6 (start), 19 lines: "המלך אחשורוש ומלכותה יתן
  המלך לרעותה הטובה / ממנה ונשמע פתגם המלך אשר יעשה בכל מלכותו / כי רבה היא וכל הנשים יתנו יקר לבעליהן למגדול /
  ועד קטן וייטב הדבר בעיני המלך והשרים ויעש / המלך כדבר ממוכן וישלח ספרים אל כל מדינות / המלך אל מדינה ומדינה
  ככתבה ואל עם ועם כלשונו / להיות כל איש שרר בביתו ומדבר כלשון עמו / אחר הדברים האלה כשך חמת המלך אחשורוש / זכר
  את ושתי ואת אשר עשתה ואת אשר נגזר / עליה ויאמרו נערי המלך משרתיו יבקשו למלך / נערות בתולות טובות מראה ויפקד
  המלך פקידים בכל / מדינות מלכותו ויקבצו את כל נערה בתולה טובת / מראה אל שושן הבירה אל בית הנשים אל יד הגא /
  סריס המלך שמר הנשים ונתון תמרוקיהן והנערה / אשר תיטב בעיני המלך תמלך תחת ושתי וייטב / הדבר בעיני המלך ויעש כן
  / איש / יהודי היה בשושן הבירה ושמו מרדכי בן יאיר בן / שמעי בן קיש איש ימיני אשר הגלה מירושלים / עם הגלה אשר
  הגלתה עם יכניה". **No divine name**: the Book of Esther famously contains none. For the checker: "אל" appears six
  times (once in line 5, twice in line 6 as "אל" and "ואל", three times in line 13) and is the preposition "to" each time ("ספרים אל כל
  מדינות", "אל מדינה ומדינה", "ואל עם ועם", "אל שושן הבירה אל בית הנשים אל יד הגא"), not the name El. "המלך" is the
  Persian king. The only other marks are a few illegible letter fragments on the inside of the loose right-hand roll.
- **2.jpg keeps** (Swedish, handwritten): "Ritning till Långfacade af Synagogbyggnaden å Mosaiska Församlingens Tomter
  N:ris 26 & 29 i 7:de Quarteret i Götheborg." (Drawing of the long front of the synagogue building on the Mosaic
  congregation's lots nos. 26 and 29 in the 7th quarter, Gothenburg); "Facade."; "Plan."; the scale "10 0 10 20 30
  Alnar."; a crowned purple monogram stamp; "August Krüger"; "Likheten med den antagna ritningen intygas. Stockholm
  den 1 Juni 1852. Ludvig Hedin" (likeness with the adopted drawing attested); the stamp "RIKSARKIVET" and the sheet
  number "1.". **No word for God in any language.** The building itself carries no lettering on this side (the
  entrance-front sheet, which shows an illegible decorative pseudo-Hebrew band over the door, was **not** used for
  that reason: its letters cannot be read, so a name could not be ruled out). **Left out:** the top-right approval
  note "I nåder gillad d. 4 maj 1852 / Öfv. Se Kongl. Brefvet af s. d.", the number "2.", and the left-margin label
  "SYNAGOGAN I GÖTEBORG. G 175:".
- **3.jpg keeps** (Singer, Hebrew p. 423, the last six lines of the prayer): "שושנת יעקב צהלה ושמחה בראותם יחד
  תכלת / מרדכי: תשועתם היית לנצח ותקותם בכל־דור ודור: / להודיע שכל־קויך לא יבשו ולא יכלמו לנצח כל־ / החוסים בך:
  ארור המן אשר בקש לאבדי. ברוך מרדכי / היהודי. ארורה זרש אשת מפחידי. ברוכה אסתר מגנה / בעדי. וגם חרבונה זכור
  לטוב:". **No divine name**: these lines speak to Hashem only as "You" (קויך, בך), as the story says. **Left out:**
  everything above, including line 3 of the page, "ידו שלח בקדושי אל" ("against God's saints", where אל is the name
  El), and the closing rubric below ("ואתה קדוש, p. 300, to ויאדיר ... קדיש ... עלינו ... ויתן לך, p. 303"; no
  name, but not needed). The crop starts at y 1695, 25 px below the vowel points of the line above (work/s-top.png),
  and ends at y 2435, above the rubric. Faint show-through of the English p. 422 on the reverse ("madest the devices
  of the crafty ... against us. Insolent in his riches ...") is barely visible; those English lines carry no word
  for God either.
- **4.jpg keeps** (Baer p. 59, No. 197 only): "Nº 197.", "Solo", "Andantino.", the Hebrew title "שושנת יעקב", the
  words "Scho-schannas ja-ă-kow zo-hă-loh w'so-me-choh bir-o-som jachad t'che-les mord'chaj t'schu-o-som ho-ji-so lo-
  nezach w'sik-wo-som b'chol dor wodor l'ho-di-a schekkol kowe-cho lo je-woschu w'lo jik-kol-mu lo-ne-zach kol ha-cho-
  sim boch.", "Leise weiter.", "W'gam charwo-noh so-chur la-tow." **No divine name.** **Left out:** No. 196 directly
  above, the blessing after the reading, headed "ב׳א יי" (the abbreviation) but sung as "Bo-ruch at-toh ă-do-noj elo-
  he-nu me-lech ho-o-lom" and again "bo-ruch at-toh ă-do-noj", the full names in transliteration; the note "Das אשר
  הניא (welches am Morgen überschlagen wird) betet man leise."; Nos. 192 to 195 above; and the rubric and footnote
  below ("Abends (ausser am מוצאי שבת) wird nun קדיש שלום, ואתה קדוש ... עלינו ... gesagt" and "*) Nach einem
  aschk'nasischen Minhag wird dieser Boruch nach der Melodie der Nº 189 gesungen."). The crop is y 942 to 1176 of the
  993 x 1404 scan: the last line of No. 196 ends above y 940; the rubric begins at about y 1180.

- **For the checker:** the current page text carries spam inserted by an anonymous edit of 2021-07-17 (revision

## 2.jpg: August Krüger's drawing of the Gothenburg synagogue, 1852

- File: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ritning_av_G%C3%B6teborgs_synagoga,_sidofasad_och_plan.jpg (5349 x
  6892, raw/ra-kruger-sidofasad.jpg). Crop 4170 x 5670 at 700, 700 (inside the ruled frame).
- Holder: Riksarkivet (National Archives of Sweden). The Commons file page, read through the API today: source
  "{{Institution:National Archives of Sweden}} [Göteborg, Synagogan. Ritningar till fasader och plan, Göteborgs
  stift, Göteborgs och Bohus län. Aug. Krüger 1852-05-04.] https://sok.riksarkivet.se/arkiv/t8odREDPgaYVbrWhIzxUa2",
  author "August Krüger", date "1852-05-04", permission "{{Riksarkivet cooperation project}}", licence "{{PD-old}}";
  extmetadata Credit "National Archives of Sweden", LicenseShortName "Public domain". (The Riksarkivet record page
  itself answered with a captcha, so its own wording was not read.)
- Public domain by age: drawn 1852; August Krüger, 1822 to 1896 (Wikidata Q20257365, "Swedish architect
  (1822-1896)"); the countersignature is Ludvig Hedin's, 1826 to 1917 (Wikidata Q5795821). Life plus 70 ended by 1
  January 1988 at the latest: PD in the US (published long before 1931 or unpublished with authors dead over 120
  years), the EU and Israel. The scan is a faithful photograph of a flat drawing and adds no copyright: US
  (Bridgeman v. Corel), EU (DSM Directive 2019/790 art. 14), Israel.
- No figures: the elevation has no staffage; the plan shows benches, stairs and the platform only. The dome finials
  carry small stars. Nothing demeaning.
- Caption facts: Hammarlund (the story's [^hammarlund], https://swedishmusicalheritage.com/composers/baer-abraham/):
  "he was recruited to the new synagogue in Gothenburg (Gothenburg), which had been consecrated in 1855", and Baer
  "was the cantor of the Gothenburg synagogue from 1857 to 1894". The drawing names itself the synagogue building
  on the Mosaic (Jewish) congregation's lots in Gothenburg, approved "I nåder gillad d. 4 maj 1852". That Krüger's
  design is the synagogue consecrated in 1855 rests on the Riksarkivet title "Göteborg, Synagogan", the English
  Wikipedia "Gothenburg Synagogue" (completed 1855 to a design by August Krüger), and the standing building, whose
  corner towers and round-arched windows match. If the lead wants the caption tighter, drop "new" and "consecrated in
  1855" and keep "Baer was its cantor from 1857 to 1894".
