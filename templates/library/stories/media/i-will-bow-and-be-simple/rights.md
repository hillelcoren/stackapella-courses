# I Will Bow and Be Simple: licence evidence for each image

Gathered 2026-11-13.

## Left out on purpose

- **Mary Hazzard's manuscript, page 108 (Winterthur Library, ASC 893).** Winterthur's record
  (https://winterthur.libraryhost.com/repositories/2/archival_objects/95) states no licence for its images, only
  "Conditions Governing Access: This collection is open and available for research." The collection record
  (https://winterthur.libraryhost.com/repositories/2/resources/7) says "This record is made available under an
  Universal 1.0 Public Domain Dedication Creative Commons license": that covers the catalogue record (metadata), not the
  page images. Not used; the manuscript is the "original" link only.
- **Andrews 1937 (The Musical Quarterly, p. 496) and Andrews 1940 (The Gift to Be Simple).** No holder states either
  page is public domain (Andrews died 1964; the 1940 book was renewed). Not used.
- **HABS photographs of the North Family at Mount Lebanon** (HABS NY,11-NELEB.V,24-*, 26-*, 30-*, 33-*). Taken by
  N. E. Baldwin (1938 to 1940) and William F. Winter, Jr. (1920s to 1931); most titles carry "Gift of New York State
  Department of Education", and the HABS index for 24-20 and 24-21 (https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/pnp/habshaer/ny/ny0100/ny0110/data/ny0110cap.pdf)
  names N. E. Baldwin, August 1939. Not made by the U.S. Government, so "images copied from other sources may be
  restricted" applies. Not used.
- **HABS photographs of the 1824 Meetinghouse at New Lebanon** (HABS NY,11-NELEB.V,3-1 to 3-17). The records seen name
  no photographer or date, and the loc.gov item pages answer 403 from here, so who made them could not be confirmed.
  Not used. A checker with a browser could open https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/ny0531.photos.115468p/ : if it
  names Jack E. Boucher, June 1962, it would be a stronger photo for this song than the schoolhouse.
- **Ministry's Shop, New Lebanon (Boucher, 1962):** fine on rights, but the building dates from 1875 (HABS NY-3291
  data, p. 7), after the song. Not used.

## 1.jpg: Shaker Schoolhouse, New Lebanon, front (west) elevation, June 1962

- Item: https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/ny0535.photos.115331p/ (HABS NY-3259).
- Call number / reproduction number: HABS NY,11-NELEB.V,10-3. Title: "Historic American Buildings Survey, June 1962,
  FRONT (WEST) ELEVATION. - Shaker Schoolhouse, Shaker Road, New Lebanon, Columbia County, NY". Author in the record:
  "Boucher, Jack E., creator".
- Downloaded: https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/pnp/habshaer/ny/ny0500/ny0535/photos/115331pu.tif (5000 x 3505).
  Cropped only to remove the film border and the hand-written "HABS # NY-3259" on the edge.
- Licence: PD-US-gov.
- Rights advisory in the item record: "No known restrictions on images made by the U.S. Government; images copied from
  other sources may be restricted.(https://www.loc.gov/rr/print/res/114_habs.html)". Seen in the LoC record as mirrored,
  field by field, at
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Historic_American_Buildings_Survey,_June_1962,_FRONT_(WEST)_ELEVATION._-_Shaker_Schoolhouse,_Shaker_Road,_New_Lebanon,_Columbia_County,_NY_HABS_NY,11-NELEB.V,10-3.tif
  (Commons licence "Public domain", PD-USGov-NPS). The www.loc.gov page itself answered 403 (bot check) from here; a
  checker with a browser should confirm the Rights Advisory line there.
- LoC HABS/HAER/HALS rights statement (https://guides.loc.gov/p-and-p-rights-and-restrictions/rights#114_habs.html, as
  quoted in the Simple Gifts set): "most of the photographs and data pages in HABS/HAER/HALS were created for the U.S.
  Government and are considered to be in the public domain", with material from other sources "noted by the presence
  of a line crediting the original source". This title carries no such line.
- The photographer was a federal employee: the National Park Service says "American documentary photographer Jack
  Boucher worked for the National Park Service from 1958 to 2006" and took "an extensive archive of images taken for
  the Historic American Buildings Survey (HABS)" (https://www.nps.gov/subjects/southfloridacollections/jack-boucher.htm).
  A building photo needs the photographer's own free licence: this one was made by a U.S. Government employee for the
  survey, so it is public domain in the US (17 U.S.C. 105). Outside the US, the Library states no known restrictions.
- No people in the picture; no trademark.
- Facts used in the caption, from HABS NY-3291 (Mount Lebanon Shakers Church Family), written data by John C.
  Poppeliers, NPS, April 1963, p. 5
  (https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/pnp/habshaer/ny/ny0500/ny0528/data/ny0528data.pdf): "The New Lebanon
  community school (HABS #NY-3259) was built around 1839 to displace family schools and still stands". Same data, p. 1:
  "The Shaker Community near New Lebanon, New York, had a unique and pre-eminent place in the Shaker world." The story
  itself: "Mary Hazzard, a sister at New Lebanon, New York, the Shakers' leading village".

## 2.jpg: A Sacred Repository of Anthems and Hymns (Canterbury, N.H., 1852), p. 195, "Celestial Choir"

- Item: https://archive.org/details/sacpository00cant (leaf n214 = printed page 195). Scan of the Princeton Theological
  Seminary Library copy, the same scan as Simple Gifts' image 1 but a different page (Simple Gifts uses leaf n20, p. 1).
  Our story cites the Harvard copy of the same edition (asacredreposito00hastgoog), a Google scan, so the Princeton scan
  was used to avoid the Google watermark.
- Downloaded: https://archive.org/download/sacpository00cant/page/n214.jpg (2347 x 3684).
- Licence: PD-old.
- Reasoning:
  - Printed at "Canterbury, N. H. 1852." No author or compiler named in print.
  - US: published 1852, before 1931, so public domain.
  - EU: an anonymous work's term is 70 years from lawful publication (1852 + 70 = 1922). Even if a compiler were credited
    (the story names Henry C. Blinn, 1824 to 1905, per the LoC name authority "Blinn, Henry Clay, 1824-1905",
    http://id.loc.gov/authorities/names/n86860470, and Marcia Hastings), anyone compiling a book in 1852 died long before 1956.
  - Israel: the same (life plus 70; anonymous works 70 years from publication).
  - A faithful scan of a flat page adds no new copyright.
- Every word on the page read (archive.org OCR, sacpository00cant_djvu.txt, checked against the image): the title
  "CELESTIAL CHOIR", verse 1 under the letter-notation tune ("Sweet spirits do surround us now, I feel them gathering
  near; I can perceive them low-ly bow, And hear their heavenly cheer"), and verses 2 to 4 ("With rapturous strains
  they swell the harp", "This living zeal they shower around, While moving in the dance", "To bow and bend be not
  ashamed, For angels bow low down; To freedom's right you can lay claim, Sweet freedom, 'tis your own"). Nothing
  demeaning; no pictures.
- Caption claims come from the story ("The Canterbury Shakers' own hymnal of 1852 sings 'To bow and bend be not
  ashamed, For angels bow low down' in a hymn about the dance" [^sacredrep]) and the page itself.

## 3.jpg: A Summary View of the Millennial Church, second edition (Albany, 1848), p. 297

- Item: https://archive.org/details/summaryviewofmil00alba (University of Toronto, Robarts Library, call number
  AFA-5927; IA metadata: "Albany, Van Benthuysen", "1848"; title page: "SECOND EDITION, REVISED AND IMPROVED.
  ALBANY: PRINTED BY C. VAN BENTHUYSEN. 1848."). A Toronto camera scan, used instead of the Google-scanned Harvard copy
  the story cites (asummaryviewmil01shakgoog), which carries a Google watermark.
- Downloaded: https://archive.org/download/summaryviewofmil00alba/page/297.jpg (1603 x 2804, the BookReader page image).
- Licence: PD-old.
- Reasoning:
  - Published Albany, 1848, second edition. The preface in this very scan is signed "CALVIN GREEN, SETH Y. WELLS. New
    Lebanon, May 12, 1823." (OCR lines 156 to 158).
  - US: published before 1931, so public domain.
  - EU and Israel (life plus 70): both authors were signing a book's preface in 1823, so both were adults then, born
    before about 1805; neither could have lived to 1956. The term has run out.
  - A faithful scan of a flat page adds no new copyright.
- Every word on the page read (OCR summaryviewofmil00alba_djvu.txt, checked against the image): the end of section 16
  (Innocence: "Those who indulge, in themselves, any feelings of lust, pride, envy, covetousness, malice, hatred or
  revenge, are not innocent before God"), section 17 "Simplicity" ("True gospel simplicity implies a godly sincerity,
  and a real singleness of heart, in all our conversation and conduct ..."), section 18, and section 19 "Meekness".
  General moral teaching; nothing that demeans any people or faith; no pictures.
- Caption quote is the story's own quote [^summaryview] ("True gospel simplicity implies a godly sincerity, and a real
  singleness of heart"), and "one of the twelve Christian virtues" follows the story and the running head "IN THE
  TWELVE CHRISTIAN VIRTUES". The story cites pp. 294 to 298, sections 5, 17, 19 and 21; this page holds 17 and 19.
