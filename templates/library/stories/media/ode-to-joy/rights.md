  ink under the baritone line (from "O Freunde" on, as on p. 113 under "Freude, schöner Götterfunken"), by an
  unknown hand at an unknown date. Dropped; only the clean title is used.
- The full 1826 title page: the royal arms' two engraved human figures. Cropped out (see 2.jpg).
- First pass dropped every van Dyke page under the Mexico rule; corrected (coordinator, 2026-11-13): he is the song's
  maker, so his pages pass, and the Poems 1911 page is now 4.jpg. The Hymnal (1911), No. 115, and the Hymnal for
  American Youth (1919), No. 47, were not added: one van Dyke page is enough, and Poems 1911 is the story's
  original and the source of our fourth line.
