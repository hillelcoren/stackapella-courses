## 1.jpg: Alford, Psalms and Hymns (London, 1844), p. 147, Hymn CXVI

- Item: https://archive.org/details/psalmsandhymnsa01unkngoog (image n167, the page the story's `original` links).
  Metadata (https://archive.org/metadata/psalmsandhymnsa01unkngoog/metadata): title "Psalms and hymns, adapted to the
  Sundays and holydays throughout the year: to which are added ...", date "1844", contributor "Oxford University",
  source http://books.google.com/books?id=x-oDAAAAQAAJ, "possible-copyright-status": "NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT",
  "copyright-region": "US".
- Downloaded https://archive.org/download/psalmsandhymnsa01unkngoog/page/n167.jpg (2217 x 3584) as
  raw/ph1844-n167.jpg. Final cropped at the top, just above the rule before "After Harvest", leaving out the end of
  the previous hymn (Hymn CXV).
- Public domain by age: printed London 1844; Henry Alford died January 1871. US: published before 1931, PD. EU and
  Israel (life plus 70): PD since 1942. A faithful scan of a flat page adds no new copyright (US: Bridgeman v. Corel;
  EU: Art. 14, DSM Directive 2019/790).
- What it shows: type only (heading, hymn number, 1 verse and 6 lines of verse 2, signature mark "o 2"). Every word
  read; nothing demeaning. The cropped-out top carried the end of Hymn CXV ("When our Israel's sore transgression /
  Stops the windows of the sky ..."), a prayer for the congregation itself, not about any people; cropped for focus.
- Caption facts: page 147, heading, number and the line "We ourselves are God's own field" from the page itself;
  Wymeswold and "earliest printing" from the story ([^ph1844]: the book's asterisk for hymns "published in this
  Collection for the first time"; [^life1873]).
