# Simple Gifts: licence evidence for each image

Gathered 2026-11-12. Winterthur images (Mary Hazzard's manuscript, ASC 893) are NOT used: Winterthur has not yet said
yes to Hillel. The manuscript appears only as the "original" link in links.md.

Note on access: www.loc.gov item pages answered 403 (a bot check) to plain requests from here. The HABS rights words
below were read (1) from the Library's own rights guide data, fetched from guides.loc.gov (the CSV the page
https://guides.loc.gov/p-and-p-rights-and-restrictions/rights loads, https://guides.loc.gov/ld.php?content_id=76336558,
row "114_habs.html"), and (2) from each item's LoC catalogue record as copied, field by field, into the Wikimedia Commons
file's raw metadata (the `rightsadvisory` field). The image files themselves came straight from the Library
(tile.loc.gov master TIFFs). A checker with a browser should open the two loc.gov item pages and confirm the
"Rights Advisory" line.

## 1.jpg: A Sacred Repository of Anthems and Hymns (Canterbury, N.H., 1852), p. 1

- Item: https://archive.org/details/sacpository00cant (page leaf n20, printed page 1, "The Faithful Witness").
  Scan of the Princeton Theological Seminary Library copy (stamped "Library of Princeton, May 12 1936, Theological
  Seminary"); IA record: "Digitized by the Internet Archive in 2012 with funding from Princeton Theological Seminary
  Library"; description "The notation uses alphabetic names of notes rather than note heads".
- Downloaded: https://archive.org/download/sacpository00cant/page/n20.jpg (2347 x 3684).
- Licence: PD-old.
- Reasoning:
  - Created and published: printed at "Canterbury, N. H. 1852." (title page). No author or compiler is named in print
    (a later hand has pencilled "H.C.B." on the title page; we do not rely on it).
  - US: published 1852, before 1931, so public domain.
  - EU: an anonymous work's term is 70 years from lawful publication (1852 + 70 = 1922). Even if a named compiler were
    credited, anyone compiling a book in 1852 was born before about 1834 and died long before 1956, so life + 70 has
    run out.
  - Israel: same reasoning (life + 70; anonymous works 70 years from publication).
  - The scan is a faithful photograph of a flat page and adds no new copyright. The IA record carries no licence
    restriction.
- Preface words used in the caption (archive.org OCR, sacpository00cant_djvu.txt, checked against the page images):
  "We are also enabled in this work, to furnish the tunes, printed in musical form, which is the first of the kind
  ever issued among Believers." and "As the gift of songs has ever been esteemed one of the most precious blessings
  ... holding a prominent place in the order of divine worship".

## 2.jpg: Sabbathday Lake Shaker Community Meetinghouse, east elevation, June 1962

- Item: https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/me0005.photos.087955p/ (survey: https://www.loc.gov/item/me0005/, HABS ME-107).
- Call number / reproduction number: HABS ME,3-SAB,1-1. Title: "Historic American Buildings Survey Gerda Peterich,
  Photographer June 1962 EAST ELEVATION - Sabbathday Lake Shaker Community Meetinghouse, West of State Route 26, South
  of North Raymond Road, northwest edge of church family area, Sabbathday Lake Village, Cumberland County, ME".
- Downloaded: https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/pnp/habshaer/me/me0000/me0005/photos/087955pu.tif (5000 x 3578).
- Licence: PD-US-gov.
- Rights advisory in the item record: "No known restrictions on images made by the U.S. Government; images copied from
  other sources may be restricted. (https://www.loc.gov/rr/print/res/114_habs.html)"
  (seen in the LoC record as mirrored at
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Historic_American_Buildings_Survey_Gerda_Peterich,_Photographer_June_1962_EAST_ELEVATION_-_Sabbathday_Lake_Shaker_Community_Meetinghouse,_West_of_State_Route_26,_South_of_North_HABS_ME,3-SAB,1-1.tif,
  Commons licence "Public domain", PD-USGov-NPS).
- LoC HABS/HAER/HALS rights statement (https://guides.loc.gov/p-and-p-rights-and-restrictions/rights#114_habs.html):
  "Material in these collections is generally considered to be in the public domain." and "The original measured
  drawings and most of the photographs and data pages in HABS/HAER/HALS were created for the U.S. Government and are
  considered to be in the public domain. However, occasionally material from a historical society or other source is
  included in the photographs or data pages. These materials are noted by the presence of a line crediting the
  original source". Requested credit: "Library of Congress, Prints & Photographs Division, HABS [...], Reproduction
  number".
- This is an original survey photograph of the building made for HABS (not a "PHOTOCOPY OF" another source; the
  survey's two photocopies, of an engraving and a watercolour, were left out). A building photo needs the
  photographer's own free licence: here the photo was made for the U.S. Government's survey, and the Library states no
  known restrictions.
- Facts used in the caption, from the HABS data pages (https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/pnp/habshaer/me/me0000/me0005/data/me0005data.pdf):
  "Date of Erection: 1794"; "Sabbathday Lake Village, New Gloucester"; "There are two entrances into the meeting
  room ... The entrance on the south side was intended for the use of the brethren, and that on the north for the
  sisters."
- Link to the song: the arranger's primary-scan notes (Hazzard ms p35, image 55): "From Mother Ann to Elder Joseph
  Brackett Dec 20th 1847 Chosen Land" (Chosen Land = New Gloucester, Maine, in the book's own spiritual names), two
  pages before "Simple Gifts." (ms p37).
