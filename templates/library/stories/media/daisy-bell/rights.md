# Daisy Bell: licence evidence for each image

Gathered 2026-11-13. Every file was downloaded with plain curl (no custom headers) and remade with sips through a BMP
round trip, which drops all metadata. No photographs on this page: all three are scans of flat printed pages.

## 1.jpg: Columbia Bicycles catalogue, 1895, p. 18, "Model 43 Tandem"

- Item page: https://archive.org/details/columbia-bicycles-1895 (metadata read from https://archive.org/metadata/columbia-bicycles-1895,
  saved as raw/meta-columbia-bicycles-1895.json). Title "Columbia Bicycles 1895"; creator "Pope Manufacturing Company";
  date 1895; collection thehoardcatalogs / catalogs; licenseurl "https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/"
  (Public Domain Mark 1.0). Description: "Columbia Bicycles, Pope Manufacturing Co., 221 Columbus Ave., Boston,
  Massachusetts, 1895. Catalogue was downloaded from Library of Congress, which has many scanned trade catalogs."
- CAVEAT for the checker: loc.gov returns 403 to plain fetches from here, so the Library of Congress's own record for
  this catalogue was not seen. The page itself is self-evidently the 1895 catalogue ("the addition to the 1895 line of
  Columbias"); the PD reasoning below does not depend on who scanned it.
- Downloaded: https://archive.org/download/columbia-bicycles-1895/page/n15.jpg (1385 x 1600), saved as
  raw/col1895-n15-full.jpg; cropped to the page (scanner border and the facing page's edge removed).
- PD reasoning:
  - US: published 1895, before 1931, so public domain.
  - EU and Israel: a corporate trade catalogue with no named author or artist, so an anonymous work: 70 years from
    publication, ended 1965. Any staff writer or draughtsman working in 1895 would in any case have died before 1956.
  - A faithful scan of a flat printed page adds no new copyright.
- Content check (every word read): heading "Model 43 Tandem, Price, $150, Weight 38 lbs."; margin slogan "Columbias
  best, therefore cheapest."; six short paragraphs on the machine (lightness, tubing, "safely carry two riders",
  adjustments, steering); page number 18. A drawing of the tandem with no riders. No people, nothing demeaning. The
  Columbia name appears only in small type, not as a logo; the brand's maker, Pope Manufacturing, is long defunct.
- Caption facts: "Model 43", "$150", "the revival of interest in this class of machine" and "the 1895 line" are all
  on the page. "Three years after the song": story, published 1892. The caption makes no claim that the song and the
  catalogue are linked.

## 2.jpg: Daisy Bell, cover (copyright 1892; this copy printed 1893 or later)

- Item page: https://archive.org/details/daisybell00harr (metadata from https://archive.org/metadata/daisybell00harr,
  saved as raw/ia-meta.json). Title "Daisy Bell"; creator "Harry Dacre"; publisher "T B Harms & Co."; date 1893;
  sponsor Public.Resource.Org; contributor Public Resource; scanned 2022-09-29 at Indiana; licenseurl
  "https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/" (Public Domain Mark 1.0). This is the story's source
  [^harms1892], image n0.
- Downloaded: https://archive.org/download/daisybell00harr/page/n0_w1600.jpg (1727 x 2296), saved as raw/ia-n0.jpg;
  the top 210 px cut off.
- PD reasoning:
  - US: published 1892 ("Copyright, MDCCCXCII, by T. B. Harms & Co."), before 1931, so public domain.
  - EU and Israel: words and music by Harry Dacre (Frank Dean), died 16 July 1922 (story: Lucerna, LoC authority
    n99283875), so public domain since 1993. The cover is type and ornament by an unnamed printer: anonymous, 70 years
    from 1892 publication, ended 1962.
  - A faithful scan of a flat printed page adds no new copyright.
- Content check: a typographic cover with ornamental borders only; no figures, no bicycle picture. Words: "Daisy Bell",
  "Song", "Written and Composed by Harry Dacre", "Song 4, Waltz 6", publishers' names and addresses, "Entered at
  Stationers' Hall, London", the copyright line, and a dealer's stamp "Howard, Farwell & Co., 20-22 & 24 West Fifth
  Street, St. Paul, Minn., Anything and Everything Musical". A pencilled "(30)" at bottom left.
- Crop: the top strip carried a former owner's handwritten name and "94, Jan." and a pencilled "179"; cut off so no
  private person's name is shown.
