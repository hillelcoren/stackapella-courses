# Lift Every Voice and Sing: rights evidence for each image

Checked 2026-11-13. Every image was opened and looked at in full: no drawn, painted, engraved or photographed human
figures, no portraits, no vignettes, no logos. No photograph in the set. Every printed and handwritten word on all four
crops was read (listed below). Nothing mocks or demeans any people or faith.

Reuse check (every stories/media/*/media.json, grep for 2021561009, 2053069, muslcdb, Stern, Rosamond): none of these
items is on another song page (the only hit, praise-to-the-lord, is "Sterndale Bennett").

Makers and life plus 100 (Mexico): all four pages carry only the makers' own work. The words are James Weldon Johnson's
(died 1938) and the music and the 1927 quartet setting J. Rosamond Johnson's (died 1954), both listed makers in
lift-every-voice.json, so these pages ride with the song page. No non-maker's text or music is on any crop: the
publishers (Stern, Marks) only print, and the dedication names Booker T. Washington without any words of his. The Yale
copy's front cover (a list of other Marks quartets, including Glow-Worm by Paul Lincke, died 1946, and a dialect title)
is not used.
