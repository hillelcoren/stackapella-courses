- Author and date (from the story's source [^alden]): William L. Alden, "Sailor Songs", vol. 65, no. 386, July 1882,
  pp. 281 to 282. Alden's dates: W. L. (William Livingston) Alden, 1837-1908 (Online Books Page author heading

  Open Library, "American lawyer, diplomat, journalist (1837-1908)"). Not to be confused with Henry Mills Alden, the

- Crop and words read: the right column only, from the top of the column to the end of the sentence "fest, and they

  demeans anyone; no figures or decoration. The left column (about Emerson and "Cheerly, men", the Liverpool packet)
  and the rest of the right column (Shallow Brown and "I'm bound away") are cropped out; I read the whole page and
  nothing on it is cruel, but the crop keeps to this song.

  at Whitby, not Watchet and not deep-water ships; kept as a spare only.
- Allan C. Green, Pera (SLV H91.108/940) under full sail: the Commons file and the SLV handle it cites point to
  different negatives (the handle's record is H91.108/939), so it is not clearly the item the record covers. Dropped.
- Allan C. Green, Hougomont (SLV H91.108/476) under full sail, out of copyright: usable, but soft focus and the record
  dates it only "[between 1900 and 1954?]". Samoena (sharp, dated ca. 1900-1912) chosen instead; Hougomont is a spare.
- Lord Templetown and Garthsnaid (SLV): at anchor or dismasted, and Garthsnaid's photographer is uncertain.
