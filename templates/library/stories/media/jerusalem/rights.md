- IA rights fields: none (no rights, licenseurl or copyright-status field), so public domain by age.

  7 October 1918, Graves vol. 2 p. 100), words by Blake (1757-1827); publisher's line "Published by J. S. Curwen &

  1988, for Rhys at the end of 2016. The 1916 copyright on the song (Parry's, later the suffrage union's and others')
