## Printings seen (read from the page images)

1. **Hymns Ancient and Modern, with Appendix** (London: printed for the proprietors by W. Clowes and Sons, 1868), "compiled and arranged under the musical editorship of William Henry Monk". University of Toronto, Faculty of Music copy: archive.org/details/hymnsancientmode00unse_0.
   - n551: Hymn 361, section "For the Young", four parts on two staves, G major (one sharp), no time signature visible, barred in four crotchets: each line a two-crotchet pickup, a bar of four, a half bar (2 + 4 + 2), double bars at the line ends; "A-men" on two minim chords (C, then G). **The source of the template.**
   - n552: the words, headed "The child Jesus", six stanzas of six lines, "Amen" after the last.
   - n14 (preface, p. ix): "Dr. Gauntlett one (a second, No. 361, harmonized for them, being inserted by leave of Mr. Masters)". So the harmony is Gauntlett's own, made for this book, and the tune had been printed before by Masters.
   - n15 (preface, end): signed only "September, 1868" (the Compilers, unnamed); thanks Mr. Monk for "general supervision".
   - n33 (Index of Tunes, p. xxviii, metre 87, 87, 77): "Irby | Dr. Gauntlett | Dr. Gauntlett | 361" (Name of Tune | Composer, or source whence taken | Harmonized or Arranged by | Hymn). No asterisk, so not a tune new to the book.
   - n24 (Index of First Lines, p. xix): "Once in royal David's city ... 87, 87, 77 ... Irby ... 361".
2. **Hymns Ancient and Modern, Revised and Enlarged Edition** (title page, n4: London: printed for the proprietors by William Clowes and Sons, Limited; undated; the catalogue gives New York, Pott, Young and Co., 1874), Harold B. 3. **Hymns for Little Children**, First American Edition (Philadelphia: Hooker, [1850]), archive.org/details/hymnsforlittlech00alex, n35 (p. 30), No. 11, headed "Who was conceived by the Holy Ghost, born of the Virgin Mary." Verses 1 and 2 read as in the 1868 book apart from capitals and punctuation ("City," "bed." "Heaven," "his shelter", "his cradle"); verse 3 has "lovely maiden" where the 1868 book has "lowly maiden" (verse 3 is not sung here). Verses 4 to 6 (n36) not looked at.

## People

- **Cecil Frances Alexander** (nee Humphreys), words. Julian, Dictionary of Hymnology (1907 edition, with the supplement): "Once in Royal David's city ... 1st appeared in her Hymns for Little Children, in 1848, p. 30 ... based on the words of the Creed ... in 6 st. of 6 l."; supplement: "Mrs. Alexander d. at Londonderry, Oct. 12, 1895." (Text from the copy used for the O Come, O Come, Emmanuel notes, ocoe-build/search/julian1907.txt.) The 1848 London first edition itself not seen: a lead. Nationality left out of makers (born in Ireland, then part of the United Kingdom).
- **Henry John Gauntlett**, tune and harmony. Grove's Dictionary (ocoe-build/search/grovesdictionary0002jafu_n7d2.txt): born 9 July 1805 at Wellington, Salop; "died suddenly, from heart disease, Feb. 21, 1876"; his publications include "Christmas Carols, 1848" and "Hymns for Little Children, 1853". The 1868 Index of Tunes names him composer and harmonizer of Irby.
- **William Henry Monk**, musical editor of Hymns Ancient and Modern (title page). Grove: "He died in London, March 1, 1889."
- **Henry Williams Baker**, chairman of the compilers: not named in this book; listed in makers on the safe side as in the O Come, O Come, Emmanuel template (died 1877), a lead from that template's notes, not checked here.

Every person died in 1925 or earlier: the rights bar is met.

## Origins as the sources state them

- Words: Alexander's Hymns for Little Children (1848), p. 30, a hymn on the Creed's "conceived by the Holy Ghost, born of the Virgin Mary" (Julian; the American edition's heading says the same).
- Tune: Gauntlett's; printed before 1868 by Masters (the 1868 preface). The leads (Christmas Carols: Four Numbers, 1849, voice and piano, slightly different words) are not checked on a scan; Grove lists "Christmas Carols, 1848". firstPublished is set to 1868 (GB), the earliest printing of words and tune together that I could read, the safe side.

## Choices

- Verses 1, 2 and 6 of six: the story (1), the stable (2) and the close (6). Verse 3 ("Christian children all must be / Mild, obedient, good as He") and verse 4 are left out for length.
- Underlay is ours (the book prints the words apart from the music): one syllable a note, each beamed quaver pair one syllable (as the beams show), and in verse 2 the line ends "low-ly" and "Ho-ly" (eight syllables where the tune has seven) take the line's last minim as two crotchets.
- Tempo 60 (kept in the 2026-11-18 review; DECISION.md has the reasons): the fastest at which a half-beat breath at each line end is half a second (singable rule 1) without moving the tune's on-beat pickups; the tune moves in quavers in every bar, so it does not drag.
- Words as the 1868 book prints them, small capitals as ordinary capitals (Jesus Christ, God, Lord, Saviour), "bed :" as "bed:".
